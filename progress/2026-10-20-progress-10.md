# Phase 8: a finite-β non-axisymmetric equilibrium, and the invariant-torus route to all-time stellarator confinement — 20 October 2026

## Outcome

Phase 8 of the [implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) adds **3 Lean modules** and two theory notes. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 15033 declarations.

- **First finite-β, non-axisymmetric toroidal equilibrium** (`Design.LandremanEquilibrium`). This formalizes family 1 of Landreman, arXiv:2609.26742 v2 (§2, eqs. 2.1–2.29; an unrefereed preprint, read and checked against the PDF).
  - ∇·B = 0, and the kernel force balance J × B = ∇p holds with J = curl B/μ₀ from actual derivatives.
  - ψ is a flux function for both B and J. Field lines are explicit harmonic oscillators, closed and confined for all time.
  - The magnetic axis is non-planar. Pressure is positive on the plasma region. The field is not axisymmetric but has 2-fold rotation and reflection symmetries, and reduces to an axisymmetric field at ε = 0.
  - **SI instance:** B₀ = 5 T, axis field 4.33–5 T, local axis β 6.25–8.33%.
  - **Idealized:** rotational transform ≡ 2 with zero shear, and every field line closed. Not coil-derived; no stability claim.
- **The invariant-torus route to all-time confinement** (stages K0–K1 of `notes/theory/kam-route.md`):
  - `Fields.invariantBarrier_forever`: a Grönwall barrier |DG·B| ≤ L|G| on a collar around {G = 0} keeps field lines on one side for all time, forward and backward.
  - `Fields.torus_trapping_forever`: an invariant torus given as a radial graph r = ρ(θ, φ) around an axis traps all field lines inside it for all time.
  - `rationalStellarator_confined_forever_of_torus`: for the exact rational stellarator field, any such torus profile with R₁ < 1/2 gives all-time confinement. This is non-vacuous at ε = 0.
  - **What remains for the actual field:** a validated approximate torus (K2, in progress) and an explicit-constant KAM theorem (K3, estimated 4–8 weeks).
- **Notes** (`notes/theory/kam-route.md`, `notes/theory/finite-beta-stellarator.md`):
  - Comparison of KAM theorems: Enciso–Luque–Peralta-Salas 2025 is best matched; Figueras–Haro–Luque 2017 has explicit constants.
  - Numerics: four Diophantine tori of the rational field, with errors near 10⁻¹⁴.
  - The finite-β landscape. The note's description of the family-1 axis was wrong and is corrected (the axis is non-planar, of constant radius √(1−ε²)).
