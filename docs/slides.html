1 · The stellarator contract

02 / 40

The aim: one candidate satisfying C1–C5 with common witnesses — not merely a non-axisymmetric field.

Tokamaks confine plasma with a large toroidal plasma current, which brings current-driven instabilities, disruptions and the need for current drive. An axisymmetric current-free field has ι = 0, so it confines nothing; a stellarator gets its rotational transform from 3-D shaping. Non-axisymmetry therefore follows from C1; it is not the target. Every result card in this deck is labelled with the clause it serves.

Defined; one current-free candidate

C1 · current-free rotational transform

Defined in Lean (netToroidalCurrent, CurrentFreeTransform) with the tokamak exclusion: a purely toroidal (current-free axisymmetric) field has ι = 0. The vacuum rational field satisfies C1 on its KAM torus (rationalStellarator_currentFreeTransform). Landreman family 2 carries positive net current and fails C1 (family2_not_currentFree).

Bridge proved; no candidate yet

C2 · omnigeneity for thermal particles

Omnigeneity is defined in the Boozer guiding-centre model, and quasisymmetry implies it (exactly, and with a drift bound linear in the defect). The current-free rational field is far from omnigenous (ε_eff ≈ 0.2 r, untrusted numerics) because its axis is planar (Garren–Boozer). Nothing certified for thermal ions and electrons on a candidate.

Test beds only

C3 · finite-β MHD stability

Mercier band on Landreman family 2 (family2_mercier_stable_derived, Mercier index supplied) and Solov'ev Mercier and ballooning; all on fields that fail C1.

Vacuum; test beds

C4 · existence

Nested certified surfaces and force balance. The vacuum field inside its KAM torus; at finite β only symmetric models and the current-carrying Landreman families.

Tokamak model and 0-D only

C5 · thermal performance

Core above burn temperature on the same candidate, with C2-derived neoclassical losses. Shown only on a Solov'ev tokamak model (transport solutions supplied) and in 0-D on stellarator parameters, with proved obstructions at measured confinement factors.

Progress so far

Plan restructured around the contract on 30 October (user-approved): goals restated, new questions Q-18–Q-22 on net current, drift and ε_eff, current-free finite β, definitions and thermal neoclassical bounds.

Claim boundary. The contract predicates (CurrentFreeTransform, BounceAveragedDrift, Omnigenous, EffectiveRippleBound, ThermalPerformance) are normative specifications in the model contracts, not yet Lean definitions.

7 · Fields and local stability

08 / 40

Maxwell identities proved; interchange stability decided both ways.

The closed-filament Biot–Savart field is divergence- and curl-free off the coil. A sharp Hardy inequality gives the Suydam criterion with both sufficiency and an explicit unstable mode.

C1/C4 · coil fields · Lean checked

∇·B = 0, ∇×B = 0

For the actual Fréchet derivative of the continuous filament integral, away from the source; curl needs a closed curve, and the open-segment curl is computed exactly.

C3 · cylinder · Lean checked

Suydam, both directions

F ≥ 0, F + 4G ≥ 0 gives a coercive margin; F + 4G < 0 gives an unstable localized mode in every neighbourhood of the resonance. Hardy's constant 4 is sharp.

C3 · cylinder · Lean checked

Newcomb certificates

Picone/Riccati/Jacobi certificates for variable coefficients, discharged by an exact rational polynomial checker running in the kernel.

Progress so far

A realistic screw pinch (a = 1 m, R₀ = 4 m, 5 T, ⟨β⟩ = 2/75) satisfies Suydam everywhere with a 25% margin, and Newcomb-reduced δW ≥ 0 is certified for every mode with 1 ≤ |m| ≤ 6 (wall at the edge); Phase 2 extended this to every integer (m, n) with m ≠ 0 (ScrewPinchModesHighM).

Claim boundary. The δW reductions are cited source results, not formalized; the result covers every mode with m ≠ 0 of this cylindrical screw pinch only. Suydam alone is necessary, not sufficient.

9 · Confinement for all time

10 / 40

Symmetry plus Noether confines every slow enough orbit forever.

Canonical momentum m⟨K,v⟩ + qψ is conserved for any Killing symmetry K; with speed conservation and an intermediate-value argument, orbits stay in a flux region for all time.

C2 alphas · tokamak model · Lean checked

Solov'ev orbits

Deuterons up to 400 keV starting in the inner region never leave the plasma torus.

C2 alphas · symmetric model · Lean checked

Helical orbits

Deuterons ≤ 30 keV and alphas ≤ 60 keV stay at r < 0.71 m inside the 4% β plasma, for all time.

Obstruction recorded

Broken symmetry

A symmetry defect δ gives confinement only for times ∝ 1/(δ|v|) — ballistic, far from slowing-down times. This bound does not cover 3.5 MeV alphas (see Phases 3–4).

Progress so far

P3 has checked full-orbit instances; field lines are confined by exact first integrals.

Claim boundary. Orbits are supplied, not constructed. Guiding-centre averaging for near-quasisymmetric fields was formalized later (AveragedTrapping: passing alphas for 0.11 s in a supplied guiding-centre model); all-time confinement without exact symmetry is not proved.

11 · Phase 1: hot enough, long enough

12 / 40

A burning plasma that provably stays above 10 keV.

A reactor-scale 0-D D-T plasma (n = 10²⁰ m⁻³, 830 m³, carbon impurities with Z_eff = 1.7) stays at or above 10 keV for all t ≥ 0 once heating beats losses at 10 keV. Radial transport obeys a proved comparison principle, so a barrier profile bounds the temperature everywhere for all time.

C5 · 0-D tokamak · Lean verified (0-D model)

Sustained burn (M1)

T ≥ 10 keV for all t ≥ 0 with P_aux ≥ 40 MW and τ_E ≥ 3.5 s whenever T ≤ 10 keV: margin +5.5 MW. Ignited variant: τ_E ≥ 5.5 s, no auxiliary power. Yield ≥ 4.8×10⁸ W × t_b.

C5 · 0-D · Obstruction recorded

Runaway is real

With constant τ_E, net heating is +77 MW at 30 keV. The window [10, 30] keV is proved only under a supplied confinement-degradation hypothesis (τ_E ≤ 1.8 s for T ≥ 30 keV).

C5 · technique · Lean verified

Transport comparison (T3)

First-crossing lemma and comparison theorems for the flux-surface heat equation with a no-flux axis: strict, margin and non-strict lower/upper barriers, and two-sided windows. D ≥ 0 suffices; backward diffusion is a counterexample.

C1/C4 · vacuum theory · Lean verified

Vacuum maximum principle (T1)

Δ|B|² = 2|∇B|² for vacuum fields; the weak maximum principle bounds the field error in the volume by its value on the surface. |B| has no interior maximum, but B = (x, y, −2z) has an interior minimum, correcting the notes.

Progress so far

13 new modules, including kernel laws for fusion power, radial transport and vacuum fields, each with sanity checks. An adversarial review caught a fuel-dilution error in the first M1 version; the kernel and the margins were corrected.

Claim boundary. Bosch–Hale reactivity enters as supplied windows (±1%). τ_E conditions are hypotheses at the crossing temperatures, not derived from a scaling law. Transport solutions are supplied, not proved to exist.

Method · In use

work status and work on

work status (or summarise work) summarizes goals, questions, the verified frontier and ready choices without builds. work on <item> runs one work unit: discovery, formalization, certificate, audit or design.

Method · In use

keep working

Autonomous mode (also keep going / keep running): pick the highest-value ready item, finish it, update notes and slides, commit and push, and continue until a user decision is needed.

Method · In use

update from workstation

Fetch workstation runs, re-check or spot-check them, merge, record and push; refresh the next workstation commands.
