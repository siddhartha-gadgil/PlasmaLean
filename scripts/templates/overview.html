Fundamentals & Physics Principles Magnetic Confinement Fusion

What is a Stellarator? Physics Concepts, Geometry & Importance

Nuclear fusion requires heating hydrogen isotopes (Deuterium & Tritium) to over 100 million degrees Celsius—far hotter than the core of the Sun. At these extreme temperatures, matter becomes fully ionized plasma. To prevent this ultra-hot plasma from touching solid walls, magnetic fields trap charged particles inside a toroidal "magnetic bottle."

A stellarator uses external three-dimensional coils to create magnetic-field twist without requiring a large inductive plasma current. This supports the goal of steady-state operation. Finite plasma currents and instability mechanisms remain possible, so confinement, stability, and engineering feasibility each need their own analysis.

🧲 Why Field Line Twist is Essential:

Simple toroidal magnetic fields cause charged particles to drift vertically due to magnetic field gradients ($\nabla B$) and curvature ($\mathbf{v}_d \propto \mathbf{B} \times \nabla B$). Ions drift up, electrons drift down, creating a huge electric field that blows the plasma apart.

By twisting magnetic field lines into a helical trajectory, a field line spends time on both top and bottom of the torus. This short-circuits the charge separation and confines the plasma!

Rotational Transform Metric:
$$\iota(\psi) = \frac{d\theta}{d\phi} = \text{Poloidal Turns per Toroidal Turn}$$

2. Tokamaks vs. Stellarators: Key Trade-offs & Physics Architecture

Why the world is shifting toward stellarators for commercial power generation.

TOKAMAK ARCHITECTURE 2D Axi-symmetric

High Current, High Disruption Risk

Uses planar toroidal coils combined with a massive central solenoid transformer to induce millions of Amperes of electric current ($I_p$) directly inside the plasma.

  • ✗ Major Disruptions: Sudden instability collapses destroy vacuum chamber walls within milliseconds.
  • ✗ Pulsed Operation: Solenoid flux runs out, requiring complex external current drive.
  • ✓ Simple Coils: Planar circular magnets are much easier to manufacture.
Primary Bottleneck: Disruption avoidance & non-inductive current drive.
STELLARATOR ARCHITECTURE 3D Non-symmetric

External Field Twist & Steady-State Potential

Uses external three-dimensional coils to supply field twist; plasma currents can also affect the operating equilibrium.

  • ✓ Reduced reliance on plasma current: Large inductive current is not required for confinement.
  • ✓ Steady-state potential: Continuous operation also requires heat removal, transport control, and component margins.
  • ⚠️ Complex Geometry: Highly complex 3D coils, and large neoclassical drift losses of thermal ions and electrons unless the field is (close to) omnigenous; quasisymmetric and quasi-isodynamic fields are special cases.
Requirement: the stellarator contract C1–C5 (below) — rotational transform from the external field with no net plasma current, omnigeneity for thermal particles, finite-β stability, existence, thermal performance. Non-axisymmetry is a consequence, not a goal.

3. The stellarator contract C1–C5

Tokamaks rely on a large toroidal plasma current, which brings kinks, tearing, disruptions and the need for current drive. PlasmaLean therefore does not aim at “non-axisymmetric” solutions as such, but at solutions with the properties a stellarator needs. A candidate satisfies the contract when, for one decoded field and equilibrium with common witnesses, all five clauses hold. Non-axisymmetry is a means and a consequence, not a goal: an axisymmetric current-free field has $\iota = 0$, so C1 already excludes tokamaks. See the implementation plan and model contracts.

The Core Challenge

Why Formal Verification (Lean 4) & Mathematical Theory are Essential

Because a stellarator lacks 2D rotational symmetry, particle orbits do not automatically conserve canonical toroidal momentum ($P_\phi$). Unoptimized 3D magnetic fields lead to large drift losses of thermal ions and electrons (neoclassical transport) and of fusion alpha particles ($\text{He}^{2+}$) before they heat the plasma.

1. Omnigeneity (contract clause C2)

The bounce-averaged radial drift of trapped thermal ions and electrons must vanish or be small (or the effective ripple $\epsilon_{\text{eff}}$ bounded). Quasisymmetry, $|B| = B(s, M\theta - N\phi)$, and quasi-isodynamic fields are special cases. Not yet defined in Lean (only weak quasisymmetry, WeakQS, is).

2. High-Dimensional Design Space

Stellarator plasma boundaries are defined by dozens of Fourier coefficients ($R_{m,n}, Z_{m,n}$). Finding stable shapes ($\delta W > 0$) requires exploring infinite-dimensional functional spaces.

3. The PlasmaLean Solution

PlasmaLean is a Lean 4 library with no project-specific axioms (the latest audit found only Lean/Mathlib's standard axioms). It already contains machine-checked equilibrium, local-stability, confinement and burn-temperature theorems for idealized models; the goal remains to let AI agents discover candidate geometries and prove precisely stated plasma-stability results for them.

Target goal: one constructible candidate with machine-checked C1–C5 on common witnesses.

PlasmaLean: Formalizing Mathematical Theory for Constructible Stellarators

The primary goal of the PlasmaLean platform is to develop new mathematical theory required to design high-performance, constructible stellarators and prove plasma stability ($\delta W > 0$) within them.

Hosted at github.com/siddhartha-gadgil/PlasmaLean, this project builds a mathematical kernel in Lean 4 without project-specific axioms. It provides machine-checked guarantees for the mathematical claims formalized so far (see the progress report), and aims at enabling AI agents and automated provers to explore high-dimensional magnetic geometries, propose stable equilibria, and synthesize proofs for Lean to check.

Kernel Layer Specifications

The Kernel: Axiom-Free Foundations of MHD Physics

The Kernel (`PlasmaLean.Kernel`) is a minimal foundation for plasma domain modeling in Lean 4, with restricted law bundles for force balance, slab and vacuum fields, fusion power and radial transport. It introduces no ad hoc project axioms; mathematical entities are represented by definitions, smooth structures, and explicit law-bearing structures grounded in Mathlib. The full 3-D manifold and differential-form setting shown below is still the target, not yet the implementation.

1. Ideal MHD System & 2-Forms

Kernel Axiom-Free

Models the continuum force balance on a compact 3-manifold $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ with boundary $\partial \Omega$. In differential forms, the solenoidal magnetic field is represented as a closed 2-form $d\beta = 0$:

Force Equilibrium: $$\mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B} = \nabla p$$
Solenoidal Field: $$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0 \equiv d\beta = 0$$
Ampère's Law: $$\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J}$$
Direct Deductions Proved in the Kernel (`field_tangency`, `current_tangency`):

$$\mathbf{B} \cdot \nabla p = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{J} \cdot \nabla p = 0$$

Magnetic field lines and current densities are everywhere tangent to scalar pressure isosurfaces $p(\mathbf{x}) = c$. By Poincaré-Hopf theorem, a connected, compact, boundaryless, orientable regular surface with a nonvanishing tangent field has genus one. A global nested foliation requires additional hypotheses.

Library code (PlasmaLean/Kernel/Equilibrium.lean, excerpt) 
-- PlasmaLean/Kernel/Equilibrium.lean (excerpt)
/-- Cartesian three-component real data. -/
abbrev Vec3 := Fin 3 → ℝ

/-- Pointwise static isotropic force balance, with SI magnetic/current/gradient data. -/
class ForceBalance (B J g : Vec3) : Prop where
  balance : J ⨯₃ B = g

/-- The magnetic field is tangent to supplied pressure-gradient data. -/
theorem field_tangency (B J g : Vec3) [ForceBalance B J g] : B ⬝ᵥ g = 0 := by
  rw [← ForceBalance.balance (B := B) (J := J) (g := g)]
  exact dot_cross_self J B

/-- The current is tangent to supplied pressure-gradient data. -/
theorem current_tangency (B J g : Vec3) [ForceBalance B J g] : J ⬝ᵥ g = 0 := by
  rw [← ForceBalance.balance (B := B) (J := J) (g := g)]
  exact dot_self_cross J B

2. Boozer Straight-Field-Line Charts

Coordinate Metric

On nested magnetic flux surfaces $\psi$, Boozer coordinates $(s, \theta, \phi)$ straighten magnetic field line trajectories. The Kernel is intended to formalize both contravariant and covariant representations (planned; so far Boozer variables appear only in the supplied guiding-centre model `BoozerGuidingCentre`):

Contravariant Representation:
$$\mathbf{B} = \nabla\psi \times \nabla\theta + \iota(\psi) \nabla\phi \times \nabla\psi$$
Covariant Representation:
$$\mathbf{B} = K \nabla\psi + I(\psi) \nabla\theta + G(\psi) \nabla\phi$$
Boozer Metric Volume Jacobian: $$\sqrt{g} = \frac{G + \iota I}{B^2}$$

Ensures non-singular coordinate transformations provided $B(\mathbf{x}) > 0$ throughout the volume.

3. External Field Generators: Measure-Theoretic Biot-Savart

To connect continuous plasma manifolds to physical coil design, the Kernel defines current distributions as 1D space curves $C_k$ carrying current $I_k$:

Parametric Biot-Savart Formula
$$\mathbf{B}_{ext}(\mathbf{r}) = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi} \oint_C \frac{d\mathbf{l} \times (\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}')}{|\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}'|^3}$$

Formulated in Lean using measure theory integrals over smooth embeddings $\gamma: S^1 \to \mathbb{R}^3$.

Formalized: Rather than relying on discrete straight-line approximations, `PlasmaLean.Theory.Fields.BiotSavart` defines the continuous filament integral over a parametrized curve in $\mathbb{R}^3$, with source-distance and derivative bounds; `Maxwell` proves $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$ and (for closed filaments) $\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = 0$ off the coil.

Boundary Field Matching: This exact continuous definition is intended to be used later to evaluate Neumann boundary matching $\mathbf{B}_{ext} \cdot \mathbf{n} = 0$ on plasma surfaces (not yet formalized).

Kernel Validation & Boundary Limits

Boundary Checks: Degeneracies & Overdetermination Proofs

To test that the Kernel definitions have the intended content and behave correctly in limiting cases, the `PlasmaLean.Sanity` suite formalizes consistent witnesses and counterexamples (equilibrium, vacuum, transport, fusion, curvature obstruction), and the strict audit modules Sanity/Audit* (AuditEquilibrium, AuditVacuum, AuditFusion, AuditTransport, AuditThermalSlab, AuditModelStability, AuditModelTransport, AuditModelGuidingCentre) add the counterexamples and risk-specific checks that the 30 October 2026 strict audit of the kernel and the model layer found missing; the limit theorems and symmetry reductions below are only partly done.

CHECK 1 $\partial / \partial \phi = 0$

Grad-Shafranov Collapse

Target (partial): under suitable exact axisymmetry assumptions ($\partial \mathbf{B} / \partial \phi = 0$), the 3D MHD Kernel equations reduce to the 2D elliptic Grad-Shafranov PDE. So far the exact Solov'ev solution is checked directly against $\mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B} = \nabla p$, and a slab Grad-Shafranov solution gives kernel force balance (`staticSlab_of_GS`); the general reduction is not yet proved:

$$\Delta^* \psi = -\mu_0 R^2 \frac{dp}{d\psi} - F(\psi)\frac{dF}{d\psi}$$
Planned role: Check that 3D contravariant metric tensors recover classical 2D tokamak theory under rotational Killing-vector assumptions (the rotational Killing field is already used in the Noether confinement theorems).
CHECK 2 $p=0, \mathbf{J}=0$

Vacuum Laplace Fields

Partial: the kernel `VacuumField` law ($\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$, $\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = 0$) is in place, the gradient of a harmonic polynomial is proved a vacuum field, and $\Delta|\mathbf{B}|^2 = 2|\nabla\mathbf{B}|^2$ gives a maximum principle. The general reduction to a scalar potential $\mathbf{B} = \nabla \phi$ satisfying Laplace's equation is not yet proved:

$$\nabla^2 \phi = 0 \quad \text{(Harmonic Potential)}$$
Role in Library: Confirms that coil-generated external vacuum fields decouple cleanly from internal plasma currents.
CHECK 3 $O(\rho^3)$ NAE

Garren-Boozer Constraint

Planned: formalize a source-specific near-axis compatibility result concerning quasisymmetry at third order in minor radius $O(\rho^3)$. (A first-order near-axis QA field is already used, as supplied data, in the near-QA alpha theorems.)

$$12 \text{ Constraints} > 10 \text{ Harmonics}$$
Planned role: Make the result's hypotheses and scope explicit and clarify when near-quasisymmetry or quasi-isodynamicity is used instead of exact symmetry.

Mathematical Theory Engine: Theory for the Stellarator Contract

Implemented so far: abstract energy bounds, Suydam (both directions), Newcomb, Mercier and ballooning criteria (including a 3-D surface ballooning reduction, not yet certified for a stellarator band), Noether confinement, an explicit-constant KAM theorem with all-time passing guiding-centre confinement over the whole passing pitch range λ ∈ [0, 1/4] (one energy) and a narrow energy window, and transport comparison. 1. Existence (C4) with a Current-Free Transform (C1)

Existence Theorem

Done: C4 for the vacuum rational stellarator field inside one KAM-certified torus (RationalStellarator.field_vacuum, rationalStellarator_confined_forever_exists), and exact finite-β non-axisymmetric equilibria (Landreman families 1 and 2). Open target (planned): $$\exists \, (\mathbf{B}, p),\; \nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0,\; \mathbf{J}\times\mathbf{B} = \nabla p,\; \beta > 0,\; |I_{\text{tor}}| \le I_{\max},\; \iota \ge \iota_{\min}$$

2. KAM Persistence of Invariant Flux Tori

KAM Surface Proof

Done: an explicit-constant KAM theorem. For the exact vacuum rational stellarator field, rationalStellarator_confined_forever_exists proves that an exact invariant torus with a Diophantine rotation number (torus A, $\iota \approx 0.137$) exists, that every point on the axis or inside it lies on a unique two-sided field line, and that these field lines stay inside the torus (within 0.19 of the axis) for all time. The Diophantine hypothesis has the form:

Diophantine Condition: $$\left|\iota(\psi) - \frac{m}{n}\right| \ge \frac{C}{n^{2+\gamma}}$$

The checked result certifies individual tori; it does not assume a complete foliation. Still open for C1: $\iota \ge \iota_{\min}$ on a nested family of certified surfaces, and finite-β versions.

3. Linear MHD Stability Spectrum & Energy Principle ($\delta W > 0$)

An energy principle relates a specified equilibrium and admissible displacement space to a precise stability conclusion. Coercivity can give norm bounds; neutral modes, boundary conditions, and continuous spectrum require separate treatment. The handbook states these obligations explicitly.

Spectral Analysis
Fixed-boundary energy integral (zero-trace displacements):
$\delta W_p = \frac{1}{2} \int_V \left[ \frac{|\mathbf{Q}|^2}{\mu_0} - \mathbf{\xi}\cdot(\mathbf{J}\times\mathbf{Q}) + \gamma p (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{\xi})^2 + (\mathbf{\xi} \cdot \nabla p)(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{\xi}) \right] d^3x$
Where $\mathbf{Q} = \nabla \times (\mathbf{\xi} \times \mathbf{B})$ is the perturbed magnetic field and $\mathbf{\kappa} = (\mathbf{b} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{b}$ is field line curvature.

arXiv:2609.26742 Grad Counterexamples (explicit)

Landreman: Analytic Toroidal 3D MHD Equilibria with Invariant Surfaces

M. Landreman (preprint, September 2026) gives explicit, elementary closed-form non-axisymmetric magnetohydrostatic equilibria with exact nested flux surfaces and smooth field, current and pressure ($\nabla p = 0$ only on the axis): family 1 with $\iota = 2$ and a non-planar axis, family 2 with sheared $\iota$. They refute Grad's conjecture as formulated by Cardona et al. (2025). The paper makes no stability or quasisymmetry claim.

• In Lean: family1_grad_counterexample (family 1: smooth MHS, nested toroidal surfaces, non-constant pressure, no continuous Euclidean symmetry; reflections open)
• Family 2: force balance, invariant surfaces, derived flux labels, Mercier band (family2_mercier_stable_derived)
• Not weakly quasisymmetric: family1_not_weakQS, family2_not_weakQS

Working with the agents: commands

PlasmaLean builds theory as well as formalizing it while designing stellarators. Work is organised as a research graph of goals (G-*), questions (Q-*) and notes (N-*) in the implementation plan. Lean is the validity gate: a result counts as established only when its named declaration compiles. The agent skills (entry point plasmalean-develop-theory) understand these commands:

CommandWhat the agent does
startStrict audit of the kernel and of changes since the last audited commit (builds and axiom check), then a status report and ready choices. No new theory on an unaudited foundation.
work status / summarise workSummary of goals, questions, the verified frontier, active and blocked notes, and a few ready choices. No builds.
work on <item>One work unit on a goal, question, note or topic: discovery, formalization, numerical certificate, audit or design, with sub-agents for independent strands.
keep workingAutonomous mode (also "keep going", "keep running"): select the highest-value ready item, finish it, update the notes and slides, commit and push, and continue until a genuine user decision is needed.
update from workstationFetch workstation runs, re-check or spot-check them on the laptop, merge, record and push; refresh the next workstation commands.

After every stage of progress the agents update the notes and registries, the progress slides and (when substantive) the progress report, regenerate this site, and commit and push. Heavy numerics and builds can run on a workstation (Workstation.md).

3. Coding Rules & Tactic Conventions for AI Agents

Rule 1: Immediate Mathlib Lemmas

Immediately following every `def` or `structure`, the agent MUST formulate and prove characterizing `@[simp]` lemmas so that downstream proof synthesis avoids unfolding raw definitions.

Rule 2: Preferred Proof Tactics

Agents should prioritize robust tactics: `aesop` for structural goals, `calc` blocks for tensor manipulation, `positivity` for norm bounds, and `gcongr` for inequalities.

Rule 3: Zero Unverified Axioms

Agents must never introduce unverified `axiom` assumptions in `PlasmaLean/Kernel/`. All physical hypotheses must be explicit fields inside Lean structures.
