Outcome

This run adds 39 certificate modules for the coil field and 8 infrastructure modules. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 26045 declarations.

  • 52 certified toroidal transits for a coil-produced stellarator field (Design.PolygonCoil.coil_stellarator_confined_concrete_N), up from 1.
  • Every field line of the coil field (a closed toroidal-field filament plus 8 closed polygonal helical filaments, 192 straight segments, from a new minimax refit) on the parameter interval [0, 8·52] that starts in the start tube stays in the confinement tube and advances by at least 2π·52.
  • Method: a cubic (4-point) tensor interpolation certificate in the frontier parameters. Fourth-derivative bounds are proved from closed-form segment kernels (Faà di Bruno). Corner values are evaluated exactly with outward-rounded square roots (≈ 20k corners).
  • Certified helical frontier error: η = 3540573·2⁻⁴⁰ ≈ 3.22×10⁻⁶, against 3.22305×10⁻⁶ allowed for 52 transits (margin 0.016–0.023%); 53 transits would need η < 2.981×10⁻⁶.
  • Independent review: sound. It checked the interpolation remainder, derivative bounds, rounding, coverage, coil avoidance and the budget arithmetic.
  • Limits: field-line existence is assumed on the interval; the coil design is untrusted (only the certificate is). Build ≈ 8 min, with ≈ 3.3 GB peak per process but up to ≈ 10 GB summed across parallel processes.
  • Generic KAM (Design.KamFlow*): the verified Newton step and iteration now take an abstract FlowField. The stellarator results are re-derived as an instance, and the regression theorems are byte-for-byte unchanged.
  • Guiding-centre groundwork:
  • Theory.Kinetic.GuidingCentreLittlejohn: the Littlejohn equations as a supplied model. For μ = 0, orbits are reparametrized field lines of the divergence-free field B + ρ_s∇×b, and all-time field-line confinement for that field transfers to the orbits (littlejohn_mu0_confined).
  • Theory.KAM.InvSqrt and PolyExprS: inverse square roots in Banach algebras via the binomial series, with Lipschitz and Taylor bounds, plus an expression language with an inverse-square-root node, as needed for |B|⁻¹ in the guiding-centre field.
  • In progress: the KAM run for the guiding-centre field, aiming at all-time confinement of passing 3.5 MeV alphas (μ = 0) in the exact stellarator field.
