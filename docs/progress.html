Outcome

This run adds 25 Lean modules. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 17885 declarations.

  • First coil-produced stellarator field with certified confinement (Design.PolygonCoil.coil_stellarator_confined_concrete).
  • Coils: a closed toroidal-field filament plus 8 closed polygonal helical filaments (128 straight segments, integer vertices at scale 2⁻³², on a winding torus of radius 0.5, stellarator-symmetric with currents K, K, −K, −K).
  • Result: every field line of the coil field on [0, 8] that starts in the start tube around the magnetic axis stays in the confinement tube and makes at least one full toroidal transit.
  • Proof:
  • an exact closed-form straight-segment Biot–Savart field, proved equal to the library's filament integral;
  • polygon corners traversed with a C¹ smoothstep parametrization;
  • the coil set shown to avoid the region (intermediate value argument on each segment);
  • a certified frontier error ‖B_helical coils − target‖ ≤ 6.5×10⁻⁴ (205 grid chunks with interpolation bounds and symmetry reduction);
  • the vacuum maximum principle and the defect-confinement chain.
  • Honest limits:
  • only one transit; the certified error sits exactly on the one-transit threshold (zero margin) and is 11.6× above the 10-transit requirement;
  • the bound is certificate-limited (the design's sampled error is ≈ 10⁻⁴);
  • the statement assumes a field line exists on [0, 8];
  • build cost: ≈ 7.5 min wall and ≈ 6 GB peak across parallel certificate modules.
  • KAM machinery for all-time confinement (stages K3b and K3c of the plan):
  • Theory.KAM.KamSequence: the quadratic-convergence lemma with loss of analyticity (kam_sequence, kam_sequence_hat) and the limit lemma (kam_limit).
  • Theory.KAM.WeightedEll1Z2: the weighted ℓ¹(ℤ²) Banach algebra, with completeness, convolution submultiplicativity, evaluation on real and complex arguments, the cohomological solver and derivatives as bounded maps, and Neumann inverses.
  • Theory.KAM.Composition and Design.KamTorusAComposition: polynomial majorant, derivative and Taylor estimates in any normed algebra; inverse bounds; the torus-A flow-form residuals proved equal to polynomial expressions, with explicit norm bounds.
  • Still remaining for all-time confinement: the weighted error and frame/twist certificates (in progress), the flux-average lemma, the Newton step, and the limit torus.
