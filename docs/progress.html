Outcome

Seven new Lean modules, containing 51 new source theorems including helper lemmas, extend the vacuum-first effort: actual filament-field differentiation, manufacturing/current error propagation, finite-thickness envelope clearance, arbitrary three-dimensional circular-coil poses, a quantitative robust probe-field certificate, and field-line escape sanity theorems.

This remains partial P2, not a practical stellarator or a construction release. The vacuum-demonstrator design basis distinguishes the checked calibration fixture from the sourced four-coil reference direction and tracks the remaining work packages. The project skills kept continuous-domain proofs, supplied physical data and actual confinement claims separate.

Baseline checkout: 1c60b91d2e13d4447a038a137816475e0e6e32da. Lean and Mathlib remain v4.34.1; no dependency or domain-kernel change. The worktree was clean before this run. No solver or floating-point result enters the logical proof path.

Implementation loops

  1. Re-audited the small equilibrium and thermal kernels, relevant planning notes and existing field certificates. No foundational correction was needed.
  2. Proved the full-domain C¹ centerline perturbation estimate, including source-distance consumption and independent current linearity.
  3. Defined conservative tube envelopes; proved zero-radius semantics, containment, all-pairs clearance and disjointness. Deliberately did not call an envelope an embedded winding pack.
  4. Composed actual-coil and current errors for the exact two-loop fixture. Proved integrability, a whole-region error budget and a nonzero probe field for the same manufactured curves.
  5. Closed the previous run's derivative-under-the-integral gap: cross-product continuous bilinear operator, local radial-kernel regularity, continuous derivative integrand and dominated differentiation on a shared source-separated neighbourhood.
  6. Instantiated the derivative result on the manufactured coils. Proved surviving tangent regularity and the zero-current derivative limit.
  7. Generalized circular coils to arbitrary rigid three-dimensional placements and propagated pose/translation errors into the two required C¹ budgets.
  8. Challenged the interpretation of the probe certificate: a field line cannot remain in this probe ball indefinitely. Proved an explicit upper bound on its residence parameter instead of claiming confinement.
  9. Reviewed primary CNT design/error-field sources and documented their evidential limits. Added a dedicated website design-basis page, linked from the retained illustrated landing page, and updated task progress.

The loops produced useful mathematics; they did not establish a practical-design endpoint or a general impossibility result. Missing hardware data and the actual nonaxisymmetric confinement certificate are not replaced by more fixture arithmetic.

Assumption and source ledger

The exact geometry, chosen tolerances and tube radii are synthetic specifications. Their arithmetic and analytic consequences are Lean checked. Permeability and currents are explicit parameters. The general integral theorem assumes full-interval continuity and source separation; the concrete tolerance theorem discharges them for the same actual curves. Supplied C¹ manufacturing hypotheses still require metrology or a parameter-based proof to describe physical hardware.

The mathematical proofs are complete elementary deductions from notes 05/06 and the following pinned Mathlib interfaces:

  • Analysis/Calculus/ParametricIntegral.lean: hasFDerivAt_integral_of_dominated_of_fderiv_le''.
  • Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Calculus.lean and Analysis/Calculus/ContDiff/Comp.lean: regularity of norm away from zero and continuity of the derivative.
  • Analysis/Normed/Operator/Bilinear.lean: continuous bilinear construction and operator bounds.
  • Analysis/Calculus/Deriv/MeanValue.lean: Convex.mul_sub_le_image_sub_of_le_deriv for the escape sanity theorem.
  • Existing project radial-kernel and continuous-integration lemmas, audited through all new public entry points.

Primary reference extraction and locators are on the design-basis page. CNT informs the next configuration and the need for metrology; its numerical/experimental results are not axioms or formal certificates for this repository. Full placement, winding, lead and operating data have not been reconstructed. Two older paper links failed to fetch; no claims were extracted from those unavailable full texts.

Next boundary

The next mathematical obligation is a source-free divergence/curl proof for the closed-filament integral, followed by finite-conductor comparison and a validated invariant surface or finite-time orbit bundle for a nonaxisymmetric configuration. The new first derivative is a prerequisite, not that Maxwell proof.

The next device obligation is a versioned four-coil geometry/current dataset and a sourced conductor/supply/thermal/support/metrology envelope. Without these, the library cannot honestly state a practical operating field, safe current or duty cycle. There is no practical stellarator qualification or completed P3–P9 claim in this run.
