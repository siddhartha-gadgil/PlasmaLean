PlasmaLean Mathematical Exposition & Contributor Suite

PlasmaLean is an attempt to design a practical stellarator using Lean 4 verification to guarantee physical, geometric, and engineering correctness. By isolating core physical contracts and sanity checks into a small, human-auditable verification kernel, both human mathematicians and AI theorem-proving agents can build machine-checked stability and coil-tolerance certificates without specification drift.

Differential & Symplectic Geometry

Hodge Theory, Beltrami Fields & Curves

Vacuum fields are harmonic 1-forms ($d\beta=0, d^*\beta=0$); force-free states are Beltrami contact forms ($\star d\beta = \lambda \beta$). Contribute proofs on Bishop frames for near-axis quasisymmetry, tubular reach bounds, and Littlejohn's guiding-center symplectic 2-form.
