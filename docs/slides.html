Progress briefing · Phase 5: one consistent plasma

01 / 17

One plasma: force balance, confined alphas, and a hot core — in a single field.

PlasmaLean is building a Lean library for stellarator design. Phase 5 proves, for one exact equilibrium field, that 3.5 MeV alphas born in the core stay confined for all time, that the core temperature stays at or above 10 keV for all time, and that the plasma pressure stays below what the equilibrium supports. Mercier stability of that equilibrium is certified, and near-quasisymmetric alphas are now held for 0.19 s.

Progress so far

G1 and G2 are joined in one theorem (solovev_one_plasma) for an axisymmetric equilibrium; Mercier stability certified there; near-QA passing alphas 0.19 s.

Claim boundary. The one-plasma theorem uses a supplied transport-geometry class (proof for the actual field in progress), a driven parameter set (Q ≈ 5.5) and a rational χ stand-in; the equilibrium is axisymmetric (tokamak-like), not a stellarator; no coils, no all-mode 3-D stability.

06 · Fields and local stability

07 / 17

Maxwell identities proved; interchange stability decided both ways.

The closed-filament Biot–Savart field is divergence- and curl-free off the coil. A sharp Hardy inequality gives the Suydam criterion with both sufficiency and an explicit unstable mode.

Lean checked

∇·B = 0, ∇×B = 0

For the actual Fréchet derivative of the continuous filament integral, away from the source; curl needs a closed curve, and the open-segment curl is computed exactly.

Lean checked

Suydam, both directions

F ≥ 0, F + 4G ≥ 0 gives a coercive margin; F + 4G < 0 gives an unstable localized mode in every neighbourhood of the resonance. Hardy's constant 4 is sharp.

Lean checked

Newcomb certificates

Picone/Riccati/Jacobi certificates for variable coefficients, discharged by an exact rational polynomial checker running in the kernel.

Progress so far

A realistic screw pinch (a = 1 m, R₀ = 4 m, 5 T, ⟨β⟩ = 2/75) satisfies Suydam everywhere with a 25% margin, and Newcomb-reduced δW ≥ 0 is certified for every mode with 1 ≤ |m| ≤ 6 (wall at the edge); Phase 2 extended this to every integer (m, n) with m ≠ 0 (ScrewPinchModesHighM).

Claim boundary. The δW reductions are cited source results, not formalized; the all-mode result is for this cylindrical screw pinch only. Suydam alone is necessary, not sufficient.

08 · Confinement for all time

09 / 17

Symmetry plus Noether confines every slow enough orbit forever.

Canonical momentum m⟨K,v⟩ + qψ is conserved for any Killing symmetry K; with speed conservation and an intermediate-value argument, orbits stay in a flux region for all time.

Lean checked

Solov'ev orbits

Deuterons up to 400 keV starting in the inner region never leave the plasma torus.

Lean checked

Helical orbits

Deuterons ≤ 30 keV and alphas ≤ 60 keV stay at r < 0.71 m inside the 4% β plasma, for all time.

Obstruction recorded

Broken symmetry

A symmetry defect δ gives confinement only for times ∝ 1/(δ|v|) — ballistic, far from slowing-down times. This bound does not cover 3.5 MeV alphas (see Phases 3–4).

Progress so far

P3 has checked full-orbit instances; field lines are confined by exact first integrals.

Claim boundary. Orbits are supplied, not constructed. Guiding-centre averaging for near-quasisymmetric fields was formalized later (AveragedTrapping: passing alphas for 0.11 s in a supplied guiding-centre model); all-time confinement without exact symmetry is not proved.

10 · Phase 1: hot enough, long enough

11 / 17

A burning plasma that provably stays above 10 keV.

A reactor-scale 0-D D-T plasma (n = 10²⁰ m⁻³, 830 m³, carbon impurities with Z_eff = 1.7) stays at or above 10 keV for all t ≥ 0 once heating beats losses at 10 keV. Radial transport obeys a proved comparison principle, so a barrier profile bounds the temperature everywhere for all time.

Lean verified (0-D model)

Sustained burn (M1)

T ≥ 10 keV for all t ≥ 0 with P_aux ≥ 40 MW and τ_E ≥ 3.5 s whenever T ≤ 10 keV: margin +5.5 MW. Ignited variant: τ_E ≥ 5.5 s, no auxiliary power. Yield ≥ 4.8×10⁸ W × t_b.

Obstruction recorded

Runaway is real

With constant τ_E, net heating is +77 MW at 30 keV. The window [10, 30] keV is proved only under a supplied confinement-degradation hypothesis (τ_E ≤ 1.8 s for T ≥ 30 keV).

Lean verified

Transport comparison (T3)

First-crossing lemma and comparison theorems for the flux-surface heat equation with a no-flux axis: strict, margin and non-strict lower/upper barriers, and two-sided windows. D ≥ 0 suffices; backward diffusion is a counterexample.

Lean verified

Vacuum maximum principle (T1)

Δ|B|² = 2|∇B|² for vacuum fields; the weak maximum principle bounds the field error in the volume by its value on the surface. |B| has no interior maximum, but B = (x, y, −2z) has an interior minimum, correcting the notes.

Progress so far

13 new modules, including kernel laws for fusion power, radial transport and vacuum fields, each with sanity checks. An adversarial review caught a fuel-dilution error in the first M1 version; the kernel and the margins were corrected.

Claim boundary. Bosch–Hale reactivity enters as supplied windows (±1%). τ_E conditions are hypotheses at the crossing temperatures, not derived from a scaling law. Transport solutions are supplied, not proved to exist.

11 · Phase 2: a core that stays hot

12 / 17

Every solution starting above the barrier keeps a 10 keV core forever.

For the flux-surface heat equation with alpha heating, bremsstrahlung and ≈70 MW of deposited heating, a certified barrier profile bounds the temperature from below for all time. A 0-D burn window now follows from the IPB98(y,2) confinement scaling instead of ad-hoc confinement-time assumptions.

Lean verified (cylinder)

Core barrier (M2)

For every supplied reactivity in the Bosch–Hale class and χ ≤ 0.5 m²/s: T ≥ T_sub ≥ 10 keV on the inner 30% of the flux, for all t ≥ 0. The barrier is a degree-10 polynomial with a 16-piece exact margin certificate.

Lean verified (0-D)

Scaling-law burn (M1)

With IPB98(y,2) for an ITER-like tokamak, P_aux ∈ [50, 70] MW and H ∈ [1.25, 2], T stays in [10, 30] keV. At nominal H the flat-profile model cannot hold 10 keV (proved). The ignition point is in (12, 13) keV and is unstable.

Lean verified (reduction supplied)

All-mode screw pinch

Newcomb stability for every integer (m, n) with m ≠ 0: stability is monotone in m², and an analytic Picone function closes the open band. Barta ballooning certificates; cylindrical Mercier ⇔ Suydam.

Lean verified

Min-B obstruction

In vacuum ∇⊥|B| = |B|κ, so no closed field line carries a transverse minimum of |B|. A straight-wire field is the witness. Coil fields are proved C^∞ off the coil.

Progress so far

14 new modules. Reviews found no soundness bugs and led to guards for shearless Mercier surfaces, a non-vacuity witness for the obstruction, and clarified χ normalization and corner compatibility.

Claim boundary. The barrier is in a straight cylinder, not a stellarator geometry; χ and density are prescribed; solutions are supplied. The kernel's D ≥ D_min > 0 had to be bypassed at the axis; Phase 3 relaxed the kernel to D ≥ 0 with a degenerate axis, making the cylinder a direct kernel instance.

12 · Phase 3: alphas, stellarator geometry, validation

13 / 17

Every 3.5 MeV alpha born in the core stays in — forever.

In an exact equilibrium satisfying J × B = ∇p at 5 T and β ≈ 4.6%, every fusion alpha with energy ≤ 3.5 MeV starting in the inner quarter of the flux, with any pitch, stays inside the plasma for all time under the full Lorentz force. The margin is 1.5%: the same argument fails at 3.7 MeV.

Lean verified (full orbits)

Alpha confinement

Noether's canonical momentum plus a rigidity bound (m s/2e ≤ 0.2694 < 0.2734 T·m). An explicit orbit shows the hypotheses can be met. The equilibrium is axisymmetric (tokamak-like).

Lean verified (averages supplied)

Hot core, helical geometry

On the exact helically symmetric equilibrium: T ≥ 10 keV on s ≤ 0.3 for all time, 63–68 MW of heating, axis pressure below the equilibrium's. The transport kernel now allows a degenerate axis (D ≥ 0). Phase 4 found this helical equilibrium Mercier-unstable, so the geometry is not MHD-stable.

Lean verified (0-D) + obstruction

Stellarator burn needs confinement

At Stellaris scale under ISS04, T stays in [10, 22] keV if f_ren ∈ [1.55, 1.75]; at measured f_ren ≤ 1.3 the flat-profile model cannot reach 10 keV (proved).

Lean verified; gap quantified

Validation & near-symmetry

Radii polynomial on ℓ¹: a nonlinear BVP solved within 10⁻⁴. Near-axis QA: passing alphas trapped 0.8 ms, with an iterated averaging lemma and gyro-Noether identity proved as the route to slowing-down times.

Progress so far

15 new modules and a recorded kernel change. Three review passes: pressure consistency of the barrier, a finite-time Boozer orbit structure, a kernel law excluding vacuous regular axes, and non-vacuity witnesses.

Claim boundary. The alpha theorem is for an axisymmetric equilibrium; near-quasisymmetric fields reached only 0.8 ms in this phase (0.11 s in Phase 4). Flux-surface averages, scaling laws, reactivity and solutions are supplied. No coil-produced configuration.

13 · Phase 4: near-quasisymmetry, validation, stability

14 / 17

From 0.8 ms to 0.11 s — and a plasma equilibrium validated to 10⁻¹¹.

Iterating an oscillatory averaging lemma three times, with exact Lie-derivative amplitudes and certified enclosures, extends passing-alpha confinement in a reactor-scale near-axis quasisymmetric field from 0.8 ms to 0.11 s. A slab Grad–Shafranov equilibrium with an exponential pressure profile is validated by the radii polynomial and proved to satisfy the kernel force balance.

Lean verified (GC model)

Near-QA alphas: 0.11 s

Passing 3.5 MeV alphas (|v∥/v| ≥ 0.9) at a = 1.7 m, 5.7 T stay inside for 0.11 s; the same gap fails at 0.12 s. Slowing down takes ≈ 0.4 s, so a ≈ 4× gap remains.

Lean verified (1-D)

Validated equilibrium

P(ψ) ∝ e^ψ − ψ: a classical C² slab equilibrium within 10⁻¹¹ of a rational candidate, unique nearby in ℓ¹, satisfying StaticSlab and ForceBalance with positive pressure.

Lean verified + negative finding

Stability findings

The general Mercier index reduces to Suydam in a cylinder. The helical equilibrium is Mercier-unstable (proved at a = 0). For the Solov'ev reactor equilibrium the true safety factor is ≥ 2.02 everywhere.

Lean verified; design recorded

Coils and coupling

Face samples certify the coil field error in a whole volume (maximum principle + surface cover). One consistent plasma needs an upper temperature barrier — and so a T-dependent diffusivity in the kernel.

Progress so far

12 new modules. Reviews found an oracle factor-of-2 error (corrected; conclusion unchanged) and no soundness defects in the averaged-trapping theorem; its ≈ 6 min build is scheduled for a proof-term rewrite.

Claim boundary. Guiding-centre model supplied; near-axis field accurate only to expansion order; passing alphas only; the validated equilibrium is a 1-D slab; the helical barrier geometry is MHD-unstable.

15 · Plan of record

16 / 17

Theory, techniques and a ladder of models — for confinement and for heat.

Each cycle develops theory, turns theorems into sound certificate checkers, and applies them to the next rung of a model ladder. Every rung must prove properties of both kinds: confinement (G1) and temperature held above burn conditions (G2).

Most tracks partly done

Theory tracks

Done in Lean: vacuum maximum principle (T1); Sturm/ballooning/Mercier (T2); parabolic comparison and barriers (T3, semilinear); 0-D power balance (T4); SBP discrete energy (T5); radii-polynomial validation, 1-D (T6); guiding centre and averaging (T8). Open: quasilinear D(T), 2-D validation, island widths/KAM (T7), bounce action J_∥, commuted energy (T9).

M0–M2 checked (idealized); M3 (slab) and M4 (0.11 s) in progress

Model ladder

M0 exact symmetric equilibria (done) → M1 0-D burning plasma → M2 radial transport on the exact geometries → M3 validated toroidal equilibrium → M4 near-axis quasisymmetry → M5 coil-produced 3-D field → M6 integrated candidate.

Applied to the notes

Corrections first

Four corrections to recent notes. Vacuum |B| has no interior maximum (minima are possible). The flux-surface line bundle is flat. 2-D ideal-MHD regularity is open. Contracts no theorem produces are vacuous.

Skill added; 6 min rewrite pending

Proof engineering

A new skill: grind and fun_induction for short proofs, bv_decide only when no native axiom results, and large certificates built as explicit proof terms (the SubsetSum pattern).

Progress so far

Phases 1–4 met their acceptance criteria in idealized models. The plan's status marks and progress log are updated after each phase.

Claim boundary. Nothing in the plan counts as evidence until a dated report records compiled Lean results.
