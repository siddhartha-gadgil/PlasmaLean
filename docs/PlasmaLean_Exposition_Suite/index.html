PlasmaLean Mathematical Exposition & Contributor Suite

PlasmaLean is a Lean 4 library working toward a verified stellarator design. Its theorems about fields, equilibria, particle orbits, stability and burn rest on a small, audited domain kernel (six modules, about 900 lines, strictly audited on 30 October 2026) and on supplied models that every statement names explicitly. The target is the stellarator contract: C1 current-free rotational transform, C2 omnigeneity for thermal ions and electrons, C3 finite-β MHD stability, C4 existence (nested surfaces, force balance), C5 thermal performance, all on one candidate. Honest status: C1 and C4 hold only for a vacuum field inside one certified torus; C3 only for current-carrying test beds; C2 and C5 on a stellarator candidate are essentially open (see the implementation plan).

Onboarding Pure Mathematicians (Topology, Differential & Complex Geometry)

Topology & Dynamical Systems

Flux Surfaces, Linking & Converse KAM

A compact regular level set of the pressure with $\nabla p \neq 0$ is a 2-torus, but nothing forces a general equilibrium to have nested flux surfaces (Grad's question; the library proves that Landreman family 1 has nested tori and no continuous symmetry). Contribute proofs on Poincaré–Hopf toroidal level sets, Gauss linking integrals for coils, and converse KAM criteria for chaotic field regions.

Differential & Symplectic Geometry

Hodge Theory, Beltrami Fields & Curves

Vacuum fields are harmonic 1-forms ($d\beta=0, d^*\beta=0$); force-free fields with constant $\lambda$ are Beltrami fields ($\star d\beta = \lambda \beta$, a contact form when $\lambda \neq 0$). Contribute proofs on Bishop frames for near-axis expansions, tubular reach bounds, and Littlejohn's guiding-centre symplectic 2-form.

Complex Geometry & Harmonic Analysis

Riemann Surfaces & Univalent Foliations

With its induced metric, each toroidal flux surface is conformally $\mathbb{C}/(\mathbb{Z} + \tau(\psi)\mathbb{Z})$ with modulus $\tau(\psi) \in \mathbb{H}$; the constant-metric case of the Hodge period bound is in the library (Theory/Geometry/TorusHodge). Contribute proofs on Radó–Kneser–Choquet univalence for flux-coordinate Jacobians and Hardy-space Fourier decay bounds.
