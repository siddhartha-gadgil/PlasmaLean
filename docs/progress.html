Outcome

Phase 3 of the implementation plan adds 15 Lean modules and one kernel change. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 4687 declarations. Headlines:

  • 3.5 MeV fusion alphas confined for all time (Design.SolovevAlpha.reactor_alpha_confined). The setting is an exact, realistically scaled Solov'ev equilibrium satisfying the kernel law J × B = ∇p (5 T on axis, β_axis = 147/3200 ≈ 4.6%, a ≈ 2.59 m). Every full Newton–Lorentz orbit of an alpha with energy ≤ 3.5 MeV, of any pitch, starting at ψ̃ ≤ ψ̃_e/4 stays inside the plasma (ψ̃ ≤ 11/12·ψ̃_e) for all t. This closes the 14 October obstruction for this tokamak-like (axisymmetric) equilibrium. The rigidity margin is 1.5%: the same argument fails at 3.7 MeV, which is proved as a rejected fixture. Non-vacuity is shown by an explicit at-rest orbit.
  • Core-temperature barrier on a stellarator-like geometry. Design.HelicalTransportBarrier.helical_core_barrier uses the exact helically symmetric equilibrium (straight stellarator, 5 T), rigorous bounds on the flux-surface averages, an edge drop in χ and 63–68 MW of deposited heating. Every solution that starts above the barrier keeps T ≥ 10 keV on s ≤ 0.3 (r ≤ 1.09 m) for all time. The barrier's axis pressure is proved below the equilibrium's. Pressure consistency across the whole plasma is impossible in this model, and the docstrings say so.
  • Kernel change (transport). The law now needs only D ≥ 0, with a regular or degenerate axis (AxisKind) and a source domain Tdom. The regular axis requires D(0) > 0. The change is recorded in the kernel file, and all theorems keep their names. The M2 cylinder barrier is now a direct kernel instance.
  • Stellarator burn window (0-D, ISS04). At Stellaris scale (a = 1.3 m, R = 12.7 m, 9 T, n = 1.5×10²⁰ m⁻³) with f_ren ∈ [1.55, 1.75] and P_aux ∈ [50, 70] MW, T ∈ [10, 22] keV for all t ≥ 0. The obstruction is also proved: at measured-level f_ren ≤ 1.3, 10 keV is not reachable in this flat-profile model.
  • Validated nonlinear BVP (M3 technique).
  • ℓ¹_ν(ℤ) is a Banach algebra; the radii-polynomial theorem is proved in three forms.
  • u″ − u + u² = (4/5)cos x has a unique solution within 10⁻⁴ of a 4-mode candidate (unique within 1/10). It is even and uniformly close to the candidate.
  • Labelled as a technique demonstration, not yet a plasma equilibrium.
  • Guiding-centre theory (M4 groundwork):
  • an exact E×B guiding centre;
  • a reduction-error bound with constant 1 (≤ ρ_L (G/B_min) s t) and a magnetic-moment drift bound;
  • a Boozer-coordinate guiding-centre model (stated as a model) with energy and helical-momentum identities, and trapping for a time ∝ 1/ε_QS;
  • the gyro-integrated Noether identity, and an iterated oscillatory averaging lemma, both with explicit constants.
  • Applied to a reactor-scale near-axis QA model, passing 3.5 MeV alphas (|v∥/v| ≥ 0.9) stay inside for 0.8 ms, about 500× short of slowing down. The iterated averaging lemma is the identified route to close the gap.
  • Ballooning on the infinite line.
  • A piecewise Barta theorem with tail certificates; the tail condition is exactly the Suydam/Mercier −1/4 threshold, and it is sharp (an explicit C¹ compactly supported negative-energy mode).
  • A certified example that is stable although Q dips below −1/4.
  • Sturm necessity: a conjugate point excludes supersolutions.

Corrections and challenged claims

  • Reviews (three lane-E passes), all addressed:
  • Pressure. Every solution covered by the first helical barrier was pressure-inconsistent with the equilibrium. The barrier was re-certified cooler, so axis consistency is now proved; whole-plasma consistency is impossible in this model and the docstrings say so.
  • Boozer orbits. BoozerGCOrbit required ψ > 0 for all time; the finite-time theorems now use an interval version.
  • Kernel. A regular axis with D(0) = 0 silently produced vacuous theorems; it is now excluded by a kernel law.
  • Smaller items. A non-vacuity orbit for the alpha theorem; a sharpness lemma for the oscillatory term; docstring precision.
  • Findings that change the plan:
  • Exact symmetry plus a realistic poloidal field suffices for 3.5 MeV alpha confinement; the earlier obstruction was the small B_pol of the ITER-like instance.
  • For near-symmetry the linear-in-ε argument cannot reach slowing-down times. The iterated averaging lemma, with interval-bounded amplitudes, is the route.
  • Stellarator burn in the 0-D flat-profile model needs f_ren above measured values, so 1-D barriers with profile peaking on stellarator geometry are the relevant G2 path.

Next boundary (Phase 4)

  1. Near-symmetric alpha confinement: bound the iterated averaging amplitudes rigorously for the near-axis QA field, aiming at ≥ 0.1 s for passing alphas; then trapped particles.
  2. Validated plasma equilibrium (M3): apply the radii polynomial to a 1-D Grad–Shafranov / force-balance ODE with a non-polynomial profile; add the C² regularity step.
  3. Couple transport and MHD: a barrier and pressure profile consistent with a chosen equilibrium family (e.g. choose the equilibrium from the barrier pressure).
  4. Mercier/ballooning for the helical and Solov'ev equilibria, using the line certificates.
  5. Coils: apply the vacuum maximum principle to coil error fields with a 2-D boundary certificate.
