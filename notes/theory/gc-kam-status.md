## 1. What is proved

### 1.1 The KAM theorem for fields with an inverse-square-root node **[Lean]**

The existing interface `FlowField` (`Design/KamFlowField`) takes polynomial field components.
`B̃` is polynomial in `(u, z, c, s)` and one algebraic variable `W = q^{−1/2}`, where
`q = R⁸|B|²`. The new interface takes expressions with the node `isqrt c d = (c + d)^{−1/2}`
(`PolyExprS`, `Theory/KAM/PolyExprS`).

- `Theory/KAM/PolyExprSStrip`: strip validity `SValid`, plus three rules:
  - chain rule `PolyExprS.dS_evalS` (for the node, `dS w = −½ w³ dS d`, derived from `w²(c + d) = 1`);
  - reality `IsReal.evalS`, `IsReal.derivS`;
  - the partial-derivative sum `deriv_eq_sumS`.
- `Design/KamFlowFieldS` (`FlowFieldS`), with three changes from `FlowField`:
  - the identities are required only at strip-valid tori (`Good`);
  - the flux primitive has a `dφ`-component `P_c`. This is needed because `α̃ = α − ρ b♭`
    contains `R b_φ dφ`, which no polynomial gauge removes;
  - the torsion numerator is defined by its formula.

  `FlowField.toS` embeds every polynomial field (`toS_resR`, `toS_Pof`), so `field14` is an
  instance.
- `Design/KamFlowFrameS`, `KamFlowStepS`, `KamFlowIterationS`: the frame, the Newton step and the
  iteration, with the same constants `SC`.
  - `FlowFieldS.StepH.kam_step` and `FlowFieldS.KamH.kam_flow_torus` are the exact torus theorems.
  - New hypotheses:
    - `GlobalH.hvR/hvZ`: the residual expressions are valid at `avec + Tq·mvec`;
    - `KamH.hvR'/hvZ'`: they are valid at `avec + 2μ(1,1,0,0,1,1)`;
    - `FrameHyp.good`.
  - The flux identity `eta2_flux` gains the term `∂_θ P_c(K)`.

### 1.3 Certificate tool **[Lean]**

`Theory/KAM/InvSqrtApprox`, **`invSqrt_approx_bound`**: an a posteriori bound for an approximate
inverse square root in `𝔸`. Assume `‖x − 1‖_ρ ≤ m < 1`, `V₀V₀⁻¹ = 1`, `‖V₀⁻² − 1‖_ρ ≤ m`,
`‖V₀ − 1‖_ρ ≤ a` with `½(1 − m)^{−3/2} m + a < 2`, and `‖V₀⁻¹‖_ρ ≤ b`. Then
`‖x^{−1/2} − V₀‖_ρ ≤ ½(1 − m)^{−3/2} b² ‖V₀²x − 1‖_ρ`.

The proof identifies `V₀` with the node value `(V₀⁻²)^{−1/2}`; the two square roots are told apart
by invertibility of `w + V₀`. This is the tool for step 1 of §3.

### 5.1 Method: ball arithmetic in the strip algebra

The residual of the GC torus is not polynomial in the Fourier data (node), and its exact
Kronecker image would have degree ≈ 40 (≈ 300 MB integers). Instead every quantity is enclosed in
a **ball** `‖X − x‖_r ≤ E` with a trigonometric-polynomial centre `x` (integer grid over `2^S`)
that is truncated and rounded after every step; each step is one kernel check.

- `Certificates/KronBall`: `phiK : ℤ[u][v] →+* 𝔸`, centres `ctr S N M w P` of weight
  `w = (d, e, p)`, the phase bridge `evH_phaseF`, the Lipschitz part `Bd_eval_sub(_below)`, the
  truncation identity `node_eq`, steps `node_step`, `norm_step`, `Bd_ctr_kron`.
- `Certificates/KronBallGrid`: packed half grids (`halfGridR`, one literal per row),
  `gridPolyNM`, static `ℓ¹` bounds, Kronecker images, reality (`isReal_ctr_sym`), derivatives
  (`dS_ctr_grid`), embedding into a larger layout (`ctr_embedGrid`), and the complete kernel steps
  `node_full`, `norm_full`, `center_full` (`nodeK`, `normK`, `centerK`).
- `Theory/KAM/PolyExprSMajQ`: kernel-computable majorants of `PolyExprS` trees (`PQS`,
  `maj_le`, `majD_le`, `majD2_le`, `nodesOK_of_all`) — used for node validity and `C₂`.

Two pipelines (oracle `scripts/gc_kam/gc_cert.py`, `ballcert.py`, `slp.py`; emitter
`emit_lean.py`; constants `gc_maj.py`):

- **high resolution** (`N = 40, M = 30`, scale `2^{-101}`, 70 variables, ≈ 60 nodes) at `K₀`:
  the residual (`e₀`) and the distances from `K₀` to the low-resolution torus;
- **low resolution** (`N = 16, M = 12`, scale `2^{-60}`, 114 variables, ≈ 110 nodes) at **every**
  torus within `μ + dist` of the low-resolution torus `K_S`, on every strip `e^r ≤ 74315/2^16`:
  `D̃, ñ` and their `u, z`-derivatives (second-derivative leaves `Design/RationalStellaratorGCPoly2`),
  `P̃`, `W₀`, the inverse-square-root node, and the torsion pieces. No Lipschitz transport is needed:
  the input balls already contain all `μ`-close tori.

The node: `V₀ ≈ (1+E)^{−1/2}` (Newton, oracle); norm nodes `‖V₀−1‖`, `‖V₀²−1‖`, `‖V₀²(1+E)−1‖`
and `invSqrt_approx_bound` give the ball of `(1+E)^{−1/2}` (`vstep`,
`Design/RationalStellaratorGCTorusCommon`); high resolution `ε_V ≤ 4.7 × 10⁻²⁴`.

- `rationalStellarator_gc_mu_confined_forever` (model units);
- `gc_mu_passing_confined_forever_SI`;
- `alpha_gc_passing_confined_forever` (3.5 MeV alphas, `B₀R₀ = 256 m v/(2e) ∈ [68.9, 69] T·m`, every `μ ∈ [0, K/(16B₀)]`).

The top module is `RationalStellaratorGCMuFamily`, which is now imported by `PlasmaLean.lean`.

**Chain.**
1. `modFieldFn (rhoE …) = gcFieldMu ρ₀ λ = gcField(ρ₀F)` pointwise, with `F = (1 − λ|B|/2)/√(1 − λ|B|)` (`Field`). The reason is that `∇ρ × b ∥ B × ∇Q`.
2. `gcFlowMu` has the flow form, with `divFree` and flux identities from split sympy certificates (`Flow`, `Instance`).
3. The torus is a Chebyshev family in `λ` of degree 12. The certificate is a family ball pipeline with three new nodes (`V₄`, `|B|`, `N = (1 − λ|B|)^{−1/2}`, the sharper step `vstep2`). It gives `e0 = 3.4e-21` and KAM smallness 0.098 (`Data`/`Check`/`Rel`/`Chain`/`Out`).
4. The majorants are uniform in `λ` via `ConstLe` (`Maj`, `Theory/KAM/PolyExprSConstMono`).
5. Exact tori come from `mu_family_torus`; flow torus and trapping from `Forever`.
6. A bootstrap (`Theory/Fields/FieldLineBootstrap`: continuity plus Grönwall uniqueness) identifies the orbit with the trapped field line of `VmuF` (`littlejohn_mu_eq_fieldLine`). This needs no a priori passing assumption.
7. SI scaling: `MuSI`, `littlejohnSI_iff`.

**Cost.** Each module was built once with `lake build`; the peak RSS is per process.
- Total for the certificate chain: about 1450 s of CPU and 1530 s of wall time (`Data*` 320 s, `CheckH1–64` 564 s, `CheckL1–8` 209 s, `RelL` about 200 s of CPU over 31 s wall).
- The upstream new modules (`Poly`, `Flow`, …) add about 225 s.
- Altogether that is about 28 CPU-minutes, with a peak of **5.5 GB** (`CheckL3`).

**Why `λ₁ = 1/16`.** `λ₁ = 1/8` (degree 15) certified numerically (`e0 = 3.6e-21`). Its double-width kernel checks (`qph`, `K3`) need 6.3–6.6 GB in a single `decide +kernel`. The packed integer has `b·W·R·K_w ≈ 238·145·109·61` bits. With `λ₁ = 1/16`, degree 12 gives the same tail (Chebyshev ratio 55 instead of 25), so `K_w` drops from 61 to 49 and memory drops by about 20%.
- Each `CheckH` module holds at most one double-width check (`LAM_HCAP=1`).
- `λ₁ = 1/4` would need degree about 21 and fails the uniform node bound.

**Extending.** A second cell `[1/16, 1/8]` (same pipeline, `lamF` shifted) would double the cost to about 56 CPU-minutes.

## 8. A positive-measure set in 3-D velocity space: the frozen-torus box (`Design/RationalStellaratorGCBox*`)

**Result [Lean].** At the fixed design value `B₀R₀ = 256 m√(2K/m)/(2e) ∈ [68.9, 69] T·m`
(`K = 3.5 MeV`, CODATA-rounded alpha mass and charge), for every starting point `x₀` within
`0.03 R₀` of the magnetic axis, the set of initial velocity vectors
`alphaBoxVel = {w ∈ ℝ³ : (1 − 2⁻⁷⁸)² K ≤ m|w|²/2 ≤ K, ⟨w, b(x₀)⟩ > 0, μ(w)B₀ ≤ (m|w|²/2)/16}`
(`μ(w) = m|w − ⟨w,b⟩b|²/(2|B(x₀)|)`, i.e. `sin²χ ≤ |B(x₀)|/(16B₀)`) has positive Lebesgue measure
in `ℝ³` (`alphaBoxVel_volume_pos`: it contains a nonempty open set), and every Littlejohn
guiding-centre orbit with `X(0) = x₀`, `v_∥(0) = ⟨w, b⟩`, `μ = μ(w)` stays inside a KAM torus
(`0.03 ≤ P ≤ 0.255`), within `0.255 R₀` of the axis, and passing, for all time; such orbits exist
(`alpha_gc_confined_forever_velocity_set`). Scope: Littlejohn model supplied, non-relativistic,
collisionless (fixed `E`, `μ`), static vacuum model field, not coil-produced.

Other declarations: `box_family_torus` (exact tori for every `(ρ, λ) ∈ rhoBox × [0, 1/16]`,
`rhoBox = [1/256 − 2⁻⁸⁶, 1/256]`), `box_family_master`, `rationalStellarator_gc_box_confined_forever`
(model units), `gc_box_passing_confined_forever_SI`, `alpha_gc_box_confined_forever` (every
`E ∈ [(1 − 2⁻⁷⁸)² K, K]`, every `μ ∈ [0, E/(16B₀)]`).

**Method: no new Chebyshev family.** The flow form is affine in the gyro-length:
`gcFlowMu ρ λ = gcFlowMu (1/256) λ + (ρ − 1/256)·F Y` (`eval_gcDM_sub`, `resR_gcFlowMu_sub`, …).
The certified pitch-angle family torus `K(λ)` of §7 is kept **frozen** for all `ρ` in the box, with
the same constants `cM`:
- residual: the certified values at `1/256` are `2.03e-21` (`R`) and `3.31e-21` (`z`) against
  `e₀ = 3.4e-21`; the perturbation is `≤ 2⁻⁸⁶·1131 ≈ 1.5e-23` (`boxResR/Z`);
- admissibility: the bounds of the low-resolution chain have slack; the nine field components move
  by `≤ 2⁻⁸⁶·30768`; `D̃⁻¹`, the torsion numerator (`DBd` bookkeeping) and the twist are
  re-bounded (`boxAdm`);
- the correction majorants (`‖F Y_D‖ ≤ 177`, …, `‖∂_u(F Y_z)‖ ≤ 30768`) are kernel-evaluated
  `PQS` mirrors, uniform in `λ` by `ConstLe` (`Bd_corr`);
- `C₂` and node validity: `ρ` enters only through a constant of modulus `≤ 1/256`, so the existing
  kernel bound covers the box by `ConstLe` (`box_C2`); this is why the box is one-sided
  (`ρ ≤ 1/256`, energies `≤ K`);
- trapping and the guiding-centre bootstrap are §7 with `1/256` replaced by `ρ`
  (`RationalStellaratorGCBoxForever`, `…BoxConfined`).

**How small.** Relative energy width `≈ 2⁻⁷⁷ ≈ 7e-24` (`≈ 2e-17 eV` at 3.5 MeV), times the full
pitch-angle range of §7. It is a genuine positive-measure set in 3-D velocity space (informally also
in phase space `(x₀, w)`; not formalised), but physically negligible in energy. A frozen torus
cannot do much better (estimate): even with `e₀` pushed to the limits of the displacement budgets
(`e₀ ≲ 1.4e-20`) the width is `≲ 10⁻²¹` relative.

**Cost.** Five modules, ≈ 18 s user CPU (24 s wall) in total, peak 2.3 GB; no heavy kernel checks
(the only kernel computations are the nine correction majorants and a few chain-value
comparisons). Suitable for the default build (not yet imported by `PlasmaLean.lean`; top module
`PlasmaLean.Design.RationalStellaratorGCBoxConfined`).

**A genuine two-parameter Chebyshev box (not done; estimates, not measured).**
- *Kronecker in a fourth variable* (`ℤ[u][v][w][y]`): every packed product grows by the number of
  `y`-slots (≥ 3 for degree 1 in `ρ`, more for nested products). The degree-12 `λ`-family already
  peaks at 5.5 GB, so staying ≤ 6 GB would force `λ`-degree ≲ 3 (a `λ`-width ≈ 10⁻⁶).
- *Evaluation/interpolation in `ρ`* (per-node values at `deg(tree)·d_ρ + 1` rational `ρ`-points,
  each a `λ`-family-sized check, followed by a Lagrange bound): memory per module stays at today's
  level, but time scales with the number of points. Taylor degree `d_ρ = 2` gives a relative
  `ρ`-half-width ≈ 4e-7 (energy ±3 eV) with ≈ 11 passes ≈ 2.5–3 CPU-h of checks plus data. `d_ρ = 3`
  (energy ±70 eV) ≈ 16 passes ≈ 4 CPU-h. A ±1% energy box needs `d_ρ ≈ 8`, ≈ 40 passes ≈ 10 CPU-h.
  All of these also need new infrastructure: a 4-variable family algebra, a two-parameter
  contraction and interpolation lemma, and a new emitter.
- Smallest feasible meaningful box within 3 CPU-h: about `d_ρ = 2`, i.e. energy width ~10⁻⁶
  relative times `λ ∈ [0, 1/16]`. This is borderline and untested.

## 9. An energy window: a genuine (energy × pitch angle) family (`Design/RationalStellaratorGCEw*`)

**Result [Lean].** At the fixed design `B₀R₀ = 256 m√(2K/m)/(2e) ∈ [68.9, 69] T·m` (`K = 3.5 MeV`),
for **every** energy `E` with `|E − K| ≤ 0.4 eV` (exactly: `E ∈ [(1 − 2⁻²⁴)²K, (1 + 2⁻²⁴)²K]`, i.e.
gyro-lengths `|ρ − 1/256| ≤ 2⁻³²`, `±0.417 eV`) and every `μ ∈ [0, E/(16B₀)]` (`λ ∈ [0, 1/16]`), there
is an exact KAM torus (`0.03 ≤ P ≤ 0.255`) trapping every passing Littlejohn guiding centre that
starts inside, for all time, and keeping it passing; such orbits exist
(**`alpha_gc_confined_forever_energy_window`**, `alpha_gc_ew_confined_forever`). The velocity set with
kinetic energy in that window, `⟨w, b⟩ > 0`, `sin²χ ≤ |B(x₀)|/(16B₀)` has positive Lebesgue measure
(**`alphaEwVel_volume_pos`**, `alpha_gc_ew_confined_forever_velocity_set`). Exact tori for every
`(ρ, λ)` of the window: `ew_family_torus`. Scope: Littlejohn model, non-relativistic, collisionless,
static vacuum model field. Relative energy width `2.4 × 10⁻⁷`, `≈ 3 × 10¹⁶` times the box of §8.
Top module (not imported by `PlasmaLean.lean`):
`lake build PlasmaLean.Design.RationalStellaratorGCEwFamily`.

**Method: evaluate at nodes, interpolate with an analytic remainder.**
- `Theory/KAM/ParamInterp` (new, generic): `hasFDerivAt_invSqrt`; `PolyExprS.differentiableAt_eval`
  (trees with inverse-square-root nodes are complex differentiable in a parameter where valid);
  `exists_coeff_bound` (Cauchy: `‖aₙ‖ ≤ M/Rⁿ`); **`norm_sub_interp_le`**
  (`‖f(h) − ∑ wⱼ f(xⱼ)‖ ≤ (1 + Λ) M (ρ/R)^m/(1 − ρ/R)` for weights exact on degree `< m`) and its
  strip-algebra form **`Bd_interp`**.
- Four node pipelines `Ew{1..4}*H` (λ-family ball pipelines, Chebyshev degree 12, `N, M = 36, 27`)
  at `ρ_j = 1/256 + t_j 2⁻³²`, `t_j ∈ {−1, −5/16, 5/16, 1}` (dyadic, Lebesgue constant `1.70`,
  proved `≤ 2` in `wEw_leb`); the field at `ρ_j` enters as the nodes `DtJ = DtF + (ρ_j − ρ₀)F Y_D/2`
  etc. Node residuals `2.03 × 10⁻²¹` (`R`), `3.31 × 10⁻²¹` (`z`) (`EwNodes`).
- The family `K(s) = ∑ ℓ_j(s) K_j` (`EwInterp`, `combEw`); the residual is affine in `ρ`, so
  `s ↦ res(1/256 + s 2⁻³²)(K(s))` is `T₀(x(s)) + 2⁻³² s T₁(x(s))` (`resR_combEw`). Coefficient norms of
  `C_k = ∑ V⁻¹_{kj} K_j` by kernel-checked integer combinations (`EwComb*`: `‖C₁‖ ≤ 7.0 × 10⁻⁹`,
  `‖C₂‖ ≤ 6.8 × 10⁻¹⁷`, `‖C₃‖ ≤ 1.8 × 10⁻²⁴` in `s`). On the complex disc `|s| ≤ 2²⁰` (complex
  `|ρ − ρ₀| ≤ 2⁻¹²`) the inputs are bounded by `aDQ` (`u ≤ 0.2005`), the trees valid (node bound
  `1.207`) and `M ≤ 6.54` (`EwDisc`). Hence `‖res‖ ≤ 2 · 3.31 × 10⁻²¹ + 1.6 × 10⁻²³ ≤ e₀ = 7 × 10⁻²¹`
  for every `|s| ≤ 1` (`ewResR_family`, `ewResZ_family`).
- Admissibility (`EwAdm`, `EwExact`): `‖K(s) − K_L‖ ≤ 7.0 × 10⁻⁹` fits the admissibility balls
  (`1.18 × 10⁻⁸`) of the **certified** low-resolution chain of §7 (re-used; its Python pickle was
  re-created bit-for-bit from the Lean data, `ew_leandata.py`); the flow moves by `≤ 2⁻³² · 30768`,
  re-bounded as in §8 with constants `cE` (`b_{n_z,u} = 0.86852`, `tT = 37.17`, `τ = 0.38`,
  `c_K ≤ 6400`, `c_L ≤ 21900`, `C₂ = 1.2 × 10¹¹` at `|ρ| ≤ 1/256 + 2⁻³²` (`EwMaj`)); smallness
  `≈ 0.26` (`EwConst`).

**Why ±0.4 eV and not ±3 eV.** The family moves by `≈ 30 h` in `u` for a gyro-length shift `h`;
`h = 2⁻³²` is the largest dyadic shift inside the certified admissibility balls. ±3 eV (`h ≈ 2⁻²⁹`,
shift `5.6 × 10⁻⁸`) needs a new low-resolution chain with balls `≈ 7 × 10⁻⁸` (tested in Python:
twist drops to `0.28`, `c_K ≈ 8350`, smallness `≈ 0.4`, still fine) and new window constants; the
interpolation itself would need the same 4 nodes (remainder `q⁴`, `q = h/R`). Not done.

**Cost (laptop, one module at a time).** 343 modules, about 70 CPU-minutes in total (node data
`≈ 22`, node kernel checks `≈ 40`, chains `≈ 4`, combinations and glue `≈ 3`), plus about 50 CPU-min
of untrusted Python per run (four node pipelines, `≈ 12 min` and `≈ 8 GB` each). **Memory:** the node
kernel checks peak at 5–8.8 GB per process (36 of 272 above 6 GB); measured the same way, the existing
`RationalStellaratorGCMuCheckH*` of §7 peak at 6.9 GB. Smaller digit-sum blocks (`FAM_LBLK=4`)
*increase* the peak (9.8 GB); larger blocks lower it only to `≈ 6.1 GB` and destroy the residual
precision. Everything else ≤ 3 GB. Workstation task: `scripts/workstation/tasks/energy-window.sh`.
