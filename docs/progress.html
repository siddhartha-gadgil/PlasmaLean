Outcome

This run adds about 440 Lean files. Almost all of them are certificate modules of two new on-demand top modules that are not imported by PlasmaLean.lean. Five modules join the default build (BallooningSurface, BallooningSecular, ParamInterp, LandremanFamily2Ballooning, LandremanFamily2BallooningSecular). The library has no project axioms. The heavy results are audited separately (scripts/check_axioms_heavy.lean).

  • Energy window: a genuine (energy × pitch-angle) family (Design.RationalStellaratorGC.alpha_gc_confined_forever_energy_window, alphaEwVel_volume_pos, alpha_gc_ew_confined_forever_velocity_set, ew_family_torus).
  • Statement: at the fixed design B₀R₀ = 256 m v/(2e) ∈ [68.9, 69] T·m, take every energy with |E − 3.5 MeV| ≤ 0.4 eV (exactly E ∈ [(1 − 2⁻²⁴)²K, (1 + 2⁻²⁴)²K], about ±0.417 eV) and every μ ∈ [0, E/(16B₀)] (λ ∈ [0, 1/16]). There is an exact KAM torus (0.03 ≤ P/R₀ ≤ 0.255) such that every passing guiding centre that starts on the axis or inside it stays inside and stays passing for all time; such orbits exist.
  • The set of initial velocities covered has positive Lebesgue measure in ℝ³ (alphaEwVel_volume_pos). The energy band is ±1.2×10⁻⁷ relative, about 3×10¹⁶ times wider than the earlier frozen-torus box (≈ 7×10⁻²⁴).
  • Method:
  • λ-families are certified at four gyro-length nodes;
  • kernel-checked integer combinations bound the coefficient norms;
  • interpolation in ρ carries a Cauchy remainder: Theory/KAM/ParamInterp (norm_sub_interp_le, Bd_interp, in the default build);
  • admissibility re-uses the certified low-resolution chain; smallness ≈ 0.26.
  • Build: on-demand, lake build PlasmaLean.Design.RationalStellaratorGCEwFamily. It has 343 modules and takes ≈ 70 CPU-minutes, plus ≈ 50 CPU-minutes of untrusted Python per run.
  • Not reached: ±3 eV. The torus family moves by ≈ 30·Δρ, so a wider window needs a re-certified low-resolution chain with larger admissibility balls.
  • Second pitch cell; λ ∈ [0, 1/8] (alpha_gc_passing_confined_forever_wide, gc_mu_passing_confined_forever_SI_wide, rationalStellarator_gc_mu_confined_forever_wide, Mu2.rationalStellarator_gc_mu_confined_forever).
  • Statement: passing 3.5 MeV alpha guiding centres with μ ∈ [0, E/(8B₀)] are confined forever and stay passing (λ|B| ≤ 3/8 is proved). Initial pitch angles satisfy sin²χ ≤ |B|/(8B₀), about 20.7° near the axis (an informal reading). This holds at one energy and one B₀R₀.
  • Certificate: Chebyshev degree 12, e₀ = 5.3×10⁻²¹, KAM smallness 0.158. Two cells cover the range (wide_cells_cover).
  • Build: on-demand, lake build PlasmaLean.Design.RationalStellaratorGCMuWide. It has 97 modules and takes 31 CPU-minutes.
  • Family-2 ballooning: reduced exactly, numerically stable, not certified (Design.LandremanFamily2Ballooning.family2_stable_of_cert, Stability.BallooningSecular.jpar_transport, Design.LandremanFamily2BallooningSecular.family2_stable_of_secular_cert).
  • Framework (Theory/Stability/BallooningSurface). It covers a general 3-D field with a flux function:
  • a transported eikonal family;
  • completeness over all θ_k (eik_complete);
  • a pointwise surface certificate implies energy ≥ 0 (eikLine_stable_of_surfaceCert).
  • Family 2: the line data are built from the actual field, and stability of every line and every θ_k reduces to one inequality on surface × ℝ. The ballooning reduction itself is supplied.
  • Exact secular part: jpar_transport proves B·∇J∥ = 2μ⟪B×∇ψ, κ⟫/|B|² for a general 3-D equilibrium. With Φ = (c₀ − J∥)s + F, stability follows from a condition free of the secular drive. Exactness is necessary, because s → ±∞ forces the s-coefficient to solve B·∇a = q₁ exactly.
  • Numerics (untrusted):
  • ballooning-stable on k ∈ [0.7, 0.9] at the physical pressure and at twice it;
  • the long-line tail goes unstable only at pressure × 2.36 (k = 0.70) to × 9.39 (k = 0.90).
  • No certificate. Certificates affine in s are infeasible. The B-spline × rational-in-s SOCP search fails the dense check at k = 0.9 at laptop resolution, and the kernel checker is not written. This is a partial, honest negative result: the band is not certified ballooning-stable.
  • Phase 15: workstation pipeline (infrastructure, no theorem). See Workstation.md.
  • Scripts:
  • scripts/workstation/run.sh runs the numerics, emits the Lean, builds each module (measuring wall time, CPU and peak memory) and runs the axiom check. It writes results and a laptop verdict, then pushes to a workstation/* branch.
  • --verify-only is the laptop re-check mode.
  • The skill plasmalean-workstation-update fetches, re-checks and merges workstation runs.
  • Smoke run: passed on the workstation hornbill (Linux x86_64, 24 CPUs): Python packages, Lean build, axioms and push.
  • Tasks registered: energy-window, lambda-cell and family2-ballooning (the last emits data only, since no checker exists).

Corrections and challenged claims

  • Memory of the default build (correction). The pitch cell λ ∈ [0, 1/16] in the default build peaks at 8.8 GB per process (RationalStellaratorGCMuCheckH1, measured with /usr/bin/time -l), not the 5.5 GB reported in the previous report. The default build therefore exceeds the 6 GB laptop target. It still builds one module at a time.
  • Both new on-demand families are also over 6 GB. In the energy window, 36 of 272 node-check modules are above 6 GB (peak 8.8 GB). In the second cell, 10 check modules are above 6 GB (peak 7.8 GB). Smaller digit-sum blocks raise the peak. Reducing kernel-check memory remains an open infrastructure item.
  • Scope of "positive measure in velocity space". The energy window gives a set of positive Lebesgue measure in ℝ³, but in a narrow energy band (±0.4 eV at 3.5 MeV) and at one B₀R₀. It is not robustness to slowing-down, which would need energies down to thermal. The physics scope is unchanged: Littlejohn guiding-centre model (supplied), non-relativistic, collisionless, static vacuum model field, not coil-produced.
  • Ballooning of the family-2 band is not claimed. Only the reduction and the secular identity are proved. The numerical margins are untrusted and are reported as evidence, not as a result.
