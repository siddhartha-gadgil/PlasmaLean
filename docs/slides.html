PlasmaLean is building a Lean library for stellarator design. 05 · What survives manufacturing uncertainty

06 / 12

Margins for the same perturbed coils and currents.

Assume closed C¹ coils with position error ≤ 1 mm, independent tangent error ≤ 1 mm/radian, and independent current errors ≤ 1% of I.

Lean checked

Field error

Actual minus nominal field has norm at most μ₀I/4 throughout the 5 cm observation ball (SI lengths in metres).

Lean checked

Nonzero probe field

The actual axial component is at least μ₀I/4 throughout the 1 mm probe ball, for μ₀ > 0 and I > 0.

Lean checked

Tube clearance

The two 5 mm-radius envelopes around actual coil centrelines remain at least 97/250 m = 388 mm apart.

Progress so far

Manufacturing and current errors compose into continuous field and clearance certificates, not just sampled checks.

Claim boundary. These tolerances are invented test inputs. Tube envelopes do not prove embedded winding packs or a finite-conductor electromagnetic model.

06 · Fields and local stability

07 / 12

Maxwell identities proved; interchange stability decided both ways.

The closed-filament Biot–Savart field is divergence- and curl-free off the coil. A sharp Hardy inequality gives the Suydam criterion with both sufficiency and an explicit unstable mode.

Lean checked

∇·B = 0, ∇×B = 0

For the actual Fréchet derivative of the continuous filament integral, away from the source; curl needs a closed curve, and the open-segment curl is computed exactly.

Lean checked

Suydam, both directions

F ≥ 0, F + 4G ≥ 0 gives a coercive margin; F + 4G < 0 gives an unstable localized mode in every neighbourhood of the resonance. Hardy's constant 4 is sharp.

Lean checked

Newcomb certificates

Picone/Riccati/Jacobi certificates for variable coefficients, discharged by an exact rational polynomial checker running in the kernel.

Progress so far

A realistic screw pinch (a = 1 m, R₀ = 4 m, 5 T, ⟨β⟩ = 2/75) satisfies Suydam everywhere with a 25% margin, and Newcomb-reduced δW ≥ 0 is certified for every mode with 1 ≤ |m| ≤ 6 (wall at the edge).

Claim boundary. The δW reductions are cited source results, not formalized; modes with |m| ≥ 7, km < 0 remain uncertified. Suydam alone is necessary, not sufficient.

Lean checked

Helical equilibrium

Helically symmetric, nonaxisymmetric exact equilibrium found by symbolic search; SI instance with pitch length 2 m, B = 5 T, β_axis = 4%.

Idealized

Claim boundary. No free-boundary or coil matching, and no global stability analysis of these equilibria yet.

08 · Confinement for all time

09 / 12

Symmetry plus Noether confines every slow enough orbit forever.

Canonical momentum m⟨K,v⟩ + qψ is conserved for any Killing symmetry K; with speed conservation and an intermediate-value argument, orbits stay in a flux region for all time.

Lean checked

Solov'ev orbits

Deuterons up to 400 keV starting in the inner region never leave the plasma torus.

Lean checked

Helical orbits

Deuterons ≤ 30 keV and alphas ≤ 60 keV stay at r < 0.71 m inside the 4% β plasma, for all time.

Obstruction recorded

Broken symmetry

A symmetry defect δ gives confinement only for times ∝ 1/(δ|v|) — ballistic, far from slowing-down times. 3.5 MeV alphas are not covered.

Progress so far

P3 has checked full-orbit instances; field lines are confined by exact first integrals.

Claim boundary. Orbits are supplied, not constructed. Quasisymmetric toroidal stellarators need guiding-centre averaging, not yet formalized.

10 · Plan of record (20 October)

11 / 12

Theory, techniques and a ladder of models — for confinement and for heat.

Each cycle develops theory, turns theorems into sound certificate checkers, and applies them to the next rung of a model ladder. Every rung must prove properties of both kinds: confinement (G1) and temperature held above burn conditions (G2).

Planned

Theory tracks

Maximum principle for vacuum fields; Sturm/ballooning; parabolic comparison and transport barriers; 0-D power balance; discrete energy (SBP); radii-polynomial validation; island widths/KAM; guiding centre and maximum-J; commuted energy.

M0 checked; M1–M6 planned

Model ladder

M0 exact symmetric equilibria (done) → M1 0-D burning plasma → M2 radial transport on the exact geometries → M3 validated toroidal equilibrium → M4 near-axis quasisymmetry → M5 coil-produced 3-D field → M6 integrated candidate.

Recorded; edits in Cycle 1

Corrections first

Four corrections to recent notes. Vacuum |B| has no interior maximum (minima are possible). The flux-surface line bundle is flat. 2-D ideal-MHD regularity is open. Contracts no theorem produces are vacuous.

Skill added

Proof engineering

A new skill: grind and fun_induction for short proofs, bv_decide only when no native axiom results, and large certificates built as explicit proof terms (the SubsetSum pattern).

Progress so far

The plan, the corrections and the skill are documents. One reference Lean module (SubsetSum) joined the build. No new physics theorem.

Claim boundary. Nothing in the plan counts as evidence until a dated report records compiled Lean results.
