Outcome

This report covers the work since report 14 (commit b2c6576, 26 October) up to the plan restructuring of 30 October (857f8f3) and the docs audit (6437b36). The library still uses standard axioms only; no project axioms were added.

  • The plan is now organized around the stellarator contract (user-approved, 30 October). A candidate must satisfy, with common witnesses: C1 current-free rotational transform (net toroidal plasma current zero or bootstrap-level, ι ≥ ι_min > 0 from the external field); C2 omnigeneity or bounded ε_eff for thermal ions and electrons; C3 MHD stability at finite β; C4 existence (nested certified surfaces, force balance); C5 thermal performance on the same candidate. Tokamaks need a large plasma current, with current-driven instabilities, disruptions and current drive; an axisymmetric current-free field has ι = 0, so C1 excludes them. Non-axisymmetry is a consequence of C1, not a goal. Solov'ev results are a tokamak model (for C5 and as technique test beds), not candidates; the Grad counterexample is mathematical infrastructure.
  • Finding recorded during the restructure (no new Lean): Landreman family 2 carries a positive net toroidal current. The compiled torCurrent_eq gives μ₀ I_tor(k₀)/2π = curK, an integral whose integrand is positive by F1_pos and Eint_pos, so I_tor > 0 for every 0 < k₀ < 19/20. Family 2 is therefore tokamak-like under C1 unless that current is shown to be bootstrap-level, and its certified Mercier-stable band does not count toward C3 for a stellarator. The positivity corollary is not yet a named declaration (Q-18).
  • Honest status against the contract:
  • C1: partial, vacuum only (the exact rational stellarator field, RationalStellarator.field_vacuum, with a KAM-certified torus of ι ≈ 0.137, rationalStellarator_confined_forever_exists).
  • C2: essentially open for thermal particles. Weak quasisymmetry is defined and the Landreman families are proved not weakly quasisymmetric; bounce-averaged drift, omnigeneity and ε_eff are specified but not in Lean. Alpha results cover one species, mostly passing orbits.
  • C3: only on test beds that fail C1 (Landreman family 2) and on the Solov'ev tokamak model.
  • C4: the vacuum field inside its certified torus; finite β only for symmetric models and the current-carrying Landreman families.
  • C5: only on a tokamak model (Solov'ev, transport solutions supplied) and in 0-D on stellarator parameters. C2 and C5 on a stellarator candidate are essentially open.
  • First strict audit (audited commit cda8bd0): all 52 kernel declarations, the model definitions behind the headline claims, repository scans and a trace of headline claims. No critical finding and no false proof. 58 kernel and 54 model sanity and regression theorems were added (Sanity/Audit*, Sanity/AuditModel*).
  • Orbit existence: Lorentz orbits exist for all time in a C¹ field; 3.5 MeV alpha orbits born in the Solov'ev reactor core exist and are all confined (closes audit MA-M2). Near-QA guiding-centre orbits exist on the full horizon for a band of births (to 0.05 s) and on an explicit ≈ 1.5 μs horizon near the axis, with proved obstructions beyond (MA-M1).
  • New theory: the corrected 1-D relative-entropy identity; the torus Hodge period inequality for constant metrics (energy ≥ |b − aτ|²/Im τ, equality iff constant); weak quasisymmetry with an axis lemma and the Landreman barrier; Landreman family 1 as a counterexample to Grad's conjecture (family1_grad_counterexample).
  • Laptop budget: the one module above 6 GB (LandremanFamily2MercierStable, 6.17–6.74 GB) is split into three modules with peaks 1.40, 2.74 and 2.65 GB.

Corrections and challenged claims

  • 6 GB target (audit M-2). Report 14 said "the 6 GB laptop target now holds everywhere". That was true of the KAM Kronecker checks (4.3 GB) but not of the default build: LandremanFamily2MercierStable peaked at 6.17 GB (6.74 GB measured later). That module is now split, with peaks ≤ 2.74 GB, so every module with a recorded peak is now within 6 GB. The four NearAxisQAAveraged parts (6.16 GB as one module before the split) built in the 26 October rebuild, but their individual peaks are not recorded.
  • decide +kernel counts (audit L-1). The slide figure "2,507 / 690" was a stage-6 count. The current counts are 1,971 overall and 130 in the default build (after Q-6).
  • Screw pinch (M-3). Stability is for every mode with m ≠ 0, with Newcomb's reduced form supplied; not "all-mode".
  • Sign of v∥ (M-1). v∥ > 0 is proved over B₀R₀ ∈ [67.95, 69.99] T·m; v∥ < 0 only at the design B₀R₀.
  • "One consistent plasma". The Solov'ev result is "one field with a pressure-budget band": the transport solution is supplied, and the pressure is bounded by a budget, not equal to the force-balance pressure. Under the contract it is a tokamak model for C5.
  • Near-QA scope (MA-M1). The near-QA results are about orbits that stay off the axis (ψ > 0). Existence on the full 0.19 s horizon is proved only for a band of births, not for the whole class.
  • Family-2 flux labels. Claims should cite family2_mercier_stable_derived (labels derived from the field), not the older family2_mercier_stable.
  • Re-labelled under the contract. Landreman non-axisymmetry and the Grad counterexample are infrastructure, not design progress; the Landreman families are C3/C4 test beds carrying net toroidal current; alpha confinement is one part of C2, for one species.
  • Website audit. Invented Lean module paths and declarations on the home page and in the Exposition suite were removed or labelled as illustrative sketches, and two mischaracterized arXiv cards were corrected (see the docs audit).

Next boundary

  1. Dommaschk QA fit (Q-16): an explicit vacuum field, current-free by construction, with certified tori, ι ≥ ι_min and Boozer/ε_QS diagnostics.
  2. C2 diagnostics on the rational field (Q-19): bounce-averaged radial drift and ε_eff on its certified surfaces.

  5. Then a current-free finite-β equilibrium (Q-20, Q-14), thermal neoclassical bounds for C5 (Q-22), and the C3 technique work transferred to it.
