The PlasmaLean Rosetta Stone

Stellarator optimization is often presented in coordinate-heavy plasma physics notation, obscuring the fact that its core objects are classic structures in differential topology, symplectic & contact geometry, and complex analysis on Riemann surfaces. Use this dictionary to translate between physics intuition, intrinsic geometry and the PlasmaLean library. Each entry says whether the concept is in the library (real declarations, with module paths, checked against the source on 30 October 2026), partly in the library (what exists, and what does not), or not in the library, in which case the Lean text is an illustrative sketch (a proposed signature) that does not compile.
