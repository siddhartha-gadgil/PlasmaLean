Outcome

This run adds 17 Lean modules and completes the KAM route. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 21297 declarations.

  • All-time confinement (Design.RationalStellarator.rationalStellarator_confined_forever). For the exact rational vacuum stellarator field B = ∇φ + ¼∇h (non-axisymmetric, two field periods, stellarator-symmetric), there is an invariant torus profile ρ with 0.077 ≤ ρ ≤ 0.19 around the magnetic axis such that every field line starting on the axis or inside the torus stays inside for all time, forward and backward.
  • rationalStellarator_exact_torus exposes the underlying object: an exact real-analytic invariant torus with rotational transform exactly ι = (75 + √5)/562, within 10⁻¹⁰ of the certified approximate torus A on a complex strip.
  • The verified KAM theorem behind it:
  • Theory.KAM.StripAlgebra, StripEval, StripSmooth: a strip-analytic Fourier algebra with polynomial majorants, Neumann inverses, derivatives (Leibniz, inverse and chain rules), the cohomological solver, uniqueness of coefficients, and C^n regularity of the limit.
  • Design.KamNewtonFrame: the frame identities, now derived, including the product-rule transport previously built into a definition; the flux-average lemma ⟨η₂⟩ = 0 exactly; approximate reducibility from the divergence-free identity.
  • Design.KamNewtonStep: one quasi-Newton step with ‖ΔK‖ ≤ c_K e/δ², ‖𝓛ΔK‖ ≤ c_L e/δ², and new error ≤ κ e²/δ⁴.
  • Design.KamIteration.KamH.kam_flow_torus: iteration to an exact solution.
  • Certified constants: γ = 0.0687, strip r = 0.1257, δ₀ = r/7, initial error 5.83×10⁻²², κ ≈ 5.7×10⁹. Smallness 32κe₀/δ₀⁴ ≈ 1.1×10⁻³ ≤ 1, a margin of about 900. Total displacement ≲ 8×10⁻¹³, against a 10⁻¹⁰ budget.
  • From the exact torus to all-time trapping (RationalStellaratorForever*): the field in cylindrical form is proved equal to the polynomials of the torus equation; the profile ρ is built by a C¹ inversion of the torus angles; the Grönwall collar bound is proved; the torus-trapping theorem is applied.
  • Independent adversarial review: sound. The reviewer traced the chain from the headline to the field definition, found no critical or medium issues, and confirmed standard axioms only.
  • Caveats (low): the theorem quantifies over two-sided field lines, and global existence of field lines inside the torus is standard but not proved here. field (1/4) is the analytic vacuum model, not a coil-produced field. One docstring states 10⁻⁶ closeness where 10⁻¹⁰ is proved (consistent, since 10⁻¹⁰ ≤ 10⁻⁶).

Scope. This is all-time confinement of magnetic field lines in an exact vacuum stellarator field. It is not particle confinement, not finite β, and not a coil-produced field (the coil field is certified for one transit). It is the library's first all-time confinement result in a genuinely non-axisymmetric field.
