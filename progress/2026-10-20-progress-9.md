# Phase 7 (part 2): ballooning on every surface, coils for the stellarator field, trapped-alpha non-resonance — 20 October 2026

## Outcome

Part 2 of Phase 7 of the [implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) adds **33 Lean modules**, many of them certificate-cell files. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 14899 declarations.

- **Ideal-ballooning stability of the reactor Solov'ev equilibrium on every flux surface** (`Design.SolovevBallooning.reactor_ballooning_all`: for all 0 < s ≤ 25 and every ballooning angle).
  - Covers the axis (handled analytically, since the certificate coefficients vanish exactly at s = 0) and the edge.
  - One certificate whose coefficients are polynomials in s covers 825 (τ, s) boxes, assembled as an explicit proof term with shared corner lemmas.
  - The build fell from ≈ 180 s (four surfaces) to ≈ 1 min (all surfaces).
  - The ballooning reduction is still supplied. Together with the certified Mercier criterion, both local ideal-MHD stability conditions now hold on the whole plasma of this equilibrium, modulo the supplied reductions.
- **Coils for the rational stellarator: the chain is proved, one input open.**
  - The toroidal-field part is realized by a concrete closed filament with certified error ≤ 2.5×10⁻⁶ on the confinement tube.
  - Any field within η of the target on the tube inherits confinement: η ≤ 5.9×10⁻⁵ gives 10 transits, η ≤ 6.6×10⁻⁴ gives 1. The defect budget is computed with a certified gradient bound ‖DÎ‖ ≤ 0.562.
  - The vacuum maximum principle transfers a frontier bound for the helical windings into the tube (`coil_stellarator_confined`, `coil_filaments_confined`).
  - **Still open:** a certified frontier bound for a concrete helical filament set. The best traced set (120 filaments) has a *sampled* error of 2.4×10⁻⁴, enough for 1 transit if certified. A Taylor-model / exact segment-field certificate is in progress.
- **Trapped alphas in exact QA: non-resonance and periodicity proved.**
  - On the start window the bounce closes within 1.6 ms with precession Δφ_b ∈ [0.7, 2.6] rad (`exactQA_bounce_precession`), so |sin Δφ_b| ≥ 1/2 and the π resonance is excluded.
  - The reference bounce is periodic, via a Lipschitz bound and Grönwall uniqueness (`exactQA_reference_bounce`).
  - **Still no trapped-alpha confinement time:** the near-QA remainder is unbounded (phase shear defeats a single global reference), and the crude amplitude bound exceeds the flux band even with zero remainder.

## Corrections and challenged claims

- Every cited declaration was grep-checked against the code.
- **Build hygiene:** the ballooning certificate was rewritten as an explicit proof-term cover (≈ 3× faster, all surfaces). The trapped-alpha cell checks (144 separate `decide +kernel` calls, ≈ 6 min) still need the same treatment.
