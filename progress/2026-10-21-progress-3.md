## Outcome

This run adds **10 Lean modules**. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 19305 declarations.

- **All quantitative inputs of the KAM theorem are certified for torus A** of the exact rational stellarator field:
  - **Weighted invariance error** (`Design.KamTorusAWeighted.torusA_weighted_error_le`): at strip radius r = 0.1257, ‖resR‖_r ≤ 5.83×10⁻²² and ‖resZ‖_r ≤ 3.465×10⁻²², with the true irrational ι (dyadic-approximation correction included). Weighted norms of the torus and its derivatives are also bounded, and D = d₀ + D′ with ‖D′‖_r ≤ 0.483.
  - **Frame** (`Design.KamTorusAFrame`): M = (∂_θK, N) with Ω(∂_θK, N) = 1 (symplectic), 0.37 ≤ det M ≤ 2.86, explicit entry bounds for M and M⁻¹, and Jacobian entries proved to be the true partial derivatives.
  - **Twist** (`Design.KamTorusA.Frame.torusA_twist`): the torus average of the 2-D twist density is ≥ 3/10 (numerically ≈ 0.399).
    - **Caveat:** the product-rule transport inside the frame-torsion definition is built into the definition and not derived. The Newton step must justify it.
  - Together with the Diophantine constant, the cohomological estimates, the convergence lemma, the ℓ¹(ℤ²) algebra and the composition bounds from parts 1–2, this completes the inputs. **The KAM Newton step, the limit torus and the final all-time confinement theorem are in progress (stage K3d); all-time confinement is not yet proved.**
- **A sheared finite-β non-axisymmetric equilibrium** (`Design.LandremanFamily2`): family 2 of Landreman arXiv:2609.26742 v2 (§3, eqs. 3.1–3.27; unrefereed).
  - The real-variable field is proved equal to the paper's complex formulas.
  - Kernel force balance with the actual current; a flux function; positive pressure; a planar non-circular elliptic magnetic axis; non-axisymmetric; field lines confined.
  - **Shear proved:** for ε = 1/4, S = 4, the rotational transform is ≤ 25/3 on ψ = 1/800 and ≥ 17/2 on ψ = 81/200 (numerically ≈ 7.98 and ≈ 11.86).
  - **Caveats:** shear is not proved for the paper's own example (ε = 2, S = 1, where it changes by only ≈ 10⁻³); field lines and angle lifts are assumed; no stability claim.
