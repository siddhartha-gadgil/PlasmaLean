New checked results

  • Topology. The signed radial volume flux across a core torus is the period of a closed one-form on the base annulus, up to the chosen orientation. A nonzero period rules out a winding-one invariant curve of the averaged field. With boundary tangency and nonsingularity, integrating the one-form instead constructs a smooth radial label ψ with values 0 and 1 on the two edges.
  • Averaging. The zero-mean periodic correction g solves X₀g = −Yψ. The corrected label ψ + εg then has derivative exactly ε²Yg for X₀ + εY. For an arbitrary proposed label, the least achievable uniform first-order residual on one orbit equals the absolute value of its mean drift.
  • Choice of phase. The flux and label descend from local charts even when the phase origin depends on position. The local corrections agree under the same rotations when the charts give the same physical period. The dynamical theorem uses a periodic coordinate representation of the total space. Embedded invariant tori

A torus parametrization that follows the field lines with an irrational transform is a C² embedding if its profiles are C², its poloidal angle winds once, and the field is locally C¹ at every point of the torus. A Grönwall bound on the distance between field lines carries coincident points forward by one turn. Irrational rotation and continuity turn a coincidence into a period of the entire section. Winding one forces that period to be a multiple of 2π. Applying the same bound to a difference quotient propagates a zero section tangent, which would force a constant section.
