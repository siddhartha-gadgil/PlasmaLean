# Phase 4 (Cycle 4): near-quasisymmetric alphas for 0.11 s, a validated plasma equilibrium, Mercier findings — 20 October 2026

## Outcome

Phase 4 of the [implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) adds **12 Lean modules**. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 5942 declarations.

- **Passing 3.5 MeV alphas stay confined for 0.11 s in a near-quasisymmetric field** (`Design.NearAxisQAAveraged.alpha_trapped_reactorQA_averaged`). The setting is the reactor-scale near-axis QA model (a = 1.7 m, 5.7 T) under the Boozer guiding-centre equations. The hypotheses are those of the Phase 3 theorem, which gave 0.8 ms; the improvement is 137×. The method:
  - three iterated oscillatory integrations by parts on the helical-momentum defect;
  - exact Lie-derivative amplitudes computed as rational polynomials;
  - bounds by certified ball enclosures and a 12×12 cover.
  - It is proved that the same gap inequality fails at 0.12 s. 0.11 s is within ≈ 4× of the 0.4 s slowing-down time.
- **First validated plasma equilibrium with a non-polynomial profile (M3).** `Design.ValidatedSlab.validated_slab_equilibrium` treats a slab Grad–Shafranov equilibrium with P(ψ) ∝ e^ψ − ψ.
  - Validated by the radii polynomial within 10⁻¹¹ in the ℓ¹(ℤ) Banach algebra, with the exponential via Mathlib's `NormedSpace.exp` and a certified series-tail enclosure.
  - The solution is proved classical C², satisfies the kernel `StaticSlab` and `ForceBalance` laws pointwise, and has positive pressure.
  - Fourier regularity (from the coefficient equation to a classical solution) also upgrades the Phase 3 BVP to a classical C² solution.
- **Mercier findings.**
  - The general Mercier index (Landreman–Jorge 2020, supplied) reduces exactly to Suydam in a cylinder.
  - **The helical equilibrium is Mercier-unstable.** This is proved in its shearless a = 0 limit; for the a = 1/4 instance used in Phase 3 it is a numerical result (magnetic hill and weak shear, margin 27–2200×). That instance should not be treated as an MHD-stable geometry, so the Phase 3 helical core barrier sits on an unstable equilibrium.
  - **For the reactor Solov'ev equilibrium the true safety factor is proved ≥ 2.02 on every surface,** derived from exact field-line winding. The exact Mercier index is positive with a magnetic well numerically; certification is pending.
- **Coil error fields from boundary data (T1).** A 2-D surface-cover theorem with a proof-term checker, plus the vacuum maximum principle, certify the field error of the two-coil fixture throughout a cube from samples on its faces. Cost grows like N² rather than N³. The interpolation constant is large (≈ 13.4 μ₀I/N).
- **Transport–MHD coupling design** (`notes/theory/transport-mhd-coupling.md`):
  - a concrete route to one consistent plasma (Solov'ev equilibrium, transport edge at the alpha-confinement shell, pressure offset, density profile);
  - the finding that pressure consistency requires an *upper* temperature barrier, which needs a temperature-dependent diffusivity (kernel extension K2).
- **Trapped alphas in near-QA:** obstructions and feasible targets recorded (`notes/theory/near-qa-trapped-particles.md`).

## Corrections and challenged claims

- **Review findings (two lane-E passes), all addressed:**
  - The helical Mercier oracle omitted a factor 1/ψ_c, so its integrals were ≈ 2× too large. It was corrected and now matches the exact Lean value at a = 0 to ≈ 10⁻¹²; the sign conclusion is unchanged.
  - The Solov'ev large-aspect-ratio Mercier statement was reworded: it records only the formula's sign given q ≥ 1 and p′ ≤ 0, and is not a certificate for the field.
  - Docstring corrections: the BVP non-triviality bound, and the Mercier sign assumptions.
- **Averaged-trapping review:** no soundness defects. The hypotheses match the Phase 3 theorem except for the longer orbit horizon. Sign persistence of v∥, the first-exit bootstrap and the enclosure soundness are all proved; the gap arithmetic was recomputed by hand. **Build cost:** 15 separate `decide +kernel` checks each recompute the same amplitude polynomial (≈ 6 min). Per the proof-engineering skill this should become a single computed literal or proof-term certificate, scheduled for Phase 5.
- **Negative finding:** the helical equilibrium used for the Phase 3 core barrier is Mercier-unstable. G1 and G2 must be stated on a geometry that is stable for both, which the coupling design addresses.
