Implementation status · 2026-10-21A Mercier-stable band of a finite-β non-axisymmetric equilibrium (Landreman family 2, λ = 200, low β; labels hand-derived) joins all-time confinement of field lines and of passing alpha guiding centres (μ = 0, B₀R₀ ∈ [67.95, 69.99] T·m) in an exact stellarator field, 52-transit coil confinement, and one consistent axisymmetric plasma with certified local stability up to ignition. Compiled declarations, build results and assumptions → Chapter notes retain their original planning context; the task progress fields report subsequent work.

Outcome

This run adds about 40 Lean modules. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 48366 declarations.

  • First Mercier-stable finite-β non-axisymmetric band (Design.LandremanFamily2Cert.family2_mercier_stable). Landreman family 2 at ε = 1/4, S = 4, λ = 200 is Mercier-stable on every flux surface k ∈ [0.7, 0.9] (ψ ∈ [0.245, 0.405], the outer part of the confined plasma), for both signs s_G and every μ₀ > 0 and pressure scale. There are no numerical hypotheses.
  • Method: validated trigonometry and quadrature (Certificates.ValidatedTrig(Fast), ValidatedQuadratureArcs), with a 34-cell cover whose soundness theorem (cellCheck_sound) is proved against the true integrals. Rejection fixtures show the checker is not vacuous (the axis, and λ = 15).
  • The equilibrium (family2_lam200_equilibrium) satisfies the kernel force balance with positive pressure.
  • Physics: numerically every surface has a magnetic hill, so stability comes from shear alone. It holds only at low β (≈ 3×10⁻⁴ on axis); the untrusted map gives no stable surface for λ ≤ 15. The tightest certified cell margin is ≈ 4×10⁻⁴ relative.
  • Review (lane E): the formal chain is sound and checker coverage and soundness are machine-checked. Medium caveat: the flux labels (ψ_t, ψ_p, V, I) and the area element entering the Mercier data are hand-derived formulas from the paper, not derived from the field in Lean (an unformalized change of variables, like the Solov'ev coarea step before it was formalized). The stabilizing shear term rests on them. The reviewer checked them numerically to 7–15 digits, and the docstrings now say so.
  • Trapped alphas (Phase 12c): a certified oscillatory kick bound M_eff = 0.0816 (window needs ≤ 0.13; the old bound was 0.2364), bounce period in [59, 72] μs, precession in [1.70, 2.49]. A long-time trapped-alpha theorem still needs the one-bounce remainders (R, e, δP) and the shears at the 10⁻³ level. A conserved-quantity comparison is proved insufficient for R, so this is parked as research.
