Outcome

This run adds 7 Lean modules toward all-time field-line confinement in the exact rational stellarator field B = ∇φ + ¼∇h. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 15690 declarations.

  • A validated approximate invariant torus (stage K2). Design.KamTorusA.torusA_error_le treats torus A, a stellarator-symmetric 2-D Fourier embedding with rational coefficients, |k| ≤ 32 and |m| ≤ 22, at rotational transform ι = (75 + √5)/562 ≈ 0.1374.
  • It solves the flow-form invariance equation with residual ≤ 1.23×10⁻²³ (R) and ≤ 6.94×10⁻²⁴ (z) for all angles, in the exact ℓ¹ norm of its Fourier coefficients. There is no truncation tail, and ι is replaced by a dyadic value within 10⁻²⁹.
  • The torus is proved to be a radial graph around the magnetic axis, 0.077 ≤ r ≤ 0.19 (torusA_graph), and the normalizing factor satisfies 0.59 ≤ D ≤ 1.42.
  • Technique: a new Kronecker-substitution certificate (Certificates.Kronecker2D), in which the exact ℓ¹ norm of a 2-D integer polynomial equals a digit sum of its 1-D image.
  • Cost: ≈ 55 s, 3.7 GB.
  • The coefficients came from an untrusted multiprecision oracle (scripts/kam_oracle/).
  • First KAM lemmas (stage K3a):
  • Theory.KAM.Diophantine: Diophantine conditions; conjugate-norm bounds for quadratic irrationals; the golden mean. Certified γ = 0.1374 (τ = 1) for torus A's transform, essentially optimal since 0.1375 fails; the frequency relevant to the Newton step has γ = 0.0687.
  • Theory.KAM.Cohomological: the cohomological equation on the 2-torus, with existence, uniqueness, a Rüssmann-type estimate ‖u‖ ≤ ‖f‖/(eγδ) in exponentially weighted ℓ¹ norms, Cauchy estimates, and a classical pointwise solution. Counterexamples show the estimate fails for rational frequencies.
  • notes/theory/kam-k3-plan.md: the full K3 statement and the nine remaining lemmas. The constant budget (Ĉ ≤ 7×10⁷ against an estimated 10⁴–10⁶) is an informal estimate, not yet certified.

All-time confinement is not yet proved. What remains is the KAM theorem itself: weighted error and frame/twist certificates, the convergence lemma and ℓ¹(ℤ²) algebra (all in progress), then the Newton step and the limit.
