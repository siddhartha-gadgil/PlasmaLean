Anatomy of a Field-Error and Clearance Certificate: the Two-Loop Calibration Fixture

Step through the five stages by which PlasmaLean proves, for a small two-loop calibration fixture, that the magnetic field stays within a stated error budget, and that the coils keep a stated clearance, for every pair of manufactured coils within a $C^1$ tolerance envelope and every current within $1\,\%$ of nominal. All declarations shown are real (abridged where marked ...); they live in Design/VacuumPair, Design/VacuumTolerance, Design/VacuumDerivative and Design/VacuumPairVolumeError. The fixture is axisymmetric: it has no rotational transform and makes no confinement claim.

Stage 1

Verification takeaway:

Evaluate the proved bounds

The theorems are stated for symbolic $\mu_0$ and $I$, with a fixed budget: $1$ mm position error, $1$ mm per radian tangent error, $1\,\%$ current error, $5$ mm tube radius. Choose a nominal current to see what the proved inequalities give in tesla (with $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}$ H/m). A different budget would need new proofs; this calculator only evaluates the existing ones.

Nominal current

Fixed by the theorems: loop radius $0.1$ m, loop planes $z = \pm 0.2$ m, observation ball radius $5$ cm, probe ball radius $1$ mm.

What the theorems give

Exact nominal midpoint field $B_z(0)$ (midpoint_field)
--
Upper bound on $\|B\|$ in the 5 cm ball (field_bound)
--
Field-error bound in the 5 cm ball (combined_field_error)
--
Lower bound on actual $B_z$ in the 1 mm probe ball (robust_probe_field)
--
Tube clearance (manufactured_tube_clearance): at least 388 mm between the two 5 mm tube envelopes, for every manufactured pair, independent of the current.
