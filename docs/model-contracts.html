Contracts

The project can prove precise implications from mathematical models and data envelopes. A practical design needs several such implications, plus evidence that the models and supplied envelopes apply to its operating regime. Lean checks the implications; experimental validation supplies evidence about the regime.

Use separate predicates, with explicit parameters:

PredicateRequired content
EquilibriumExistsSolution space, equations, boundary conditions, constraints, and distance from a numerical candidate
FieldLineBarrierEmbedded invariant separating surface for the stated magnetic field
ParticleConfinedUntilFull-orbit or reduced dynamics, initial set, spatial region, time horizon, and reduction error
LinearMHDStableEquilibrium, admissible displacement space, boundary/wall model, controlled norm or observable, and stability definition
NonlinearStableEvolution model, solution class, initial neighborhood, norm/modulo symmetries, and interval of existence
TransportBoundSpecies, collision/turbulence model, profiles, heat/particle flux, and averaging interval
ComponentFeasibleGeometry, loads, constitutive data, tolerances, temperatures, degradation, and duty cycle
DesignFeasibleExplicit conjunction of the required contracts for the same candidate and operating states

Avoid an unqualified Stable : Prop encompassing all these meanings. In particular, linearly stable ideal MHD is compatible with kinetic microinstabilities and poor confinement.

Record each assumption's origin: mathematical theorem; chosen model law; measured datum or empirical fit; machine requirement; or unresolved research hypothesis. Keep arithmetic error, discretization error, parameter/data uncertainty, and model discrepancy separate.

Architecture

Preserve the existing kernel → sanity checks → verified library architecture. The domain kernel is a set of definitions and law interfaces, distinct from Lean's logical kernel.

Suggested module groups, all initially inside the existing PlasmaLean library:

GroupPurpose
KernelEuclidean domains, fields, species, physical constants, boundaries, model predicates, material-law interfaces
SanityWitnesses, limits, units, symmetry, conservation, and counterexamples to overbroad definitions
Theory.Geometry / Equilibrium / Stability / KineticDerived mathematics
Theory.EngineeringThermal, mechanical, electromagnetic, irradiation, and lifetime consequences
CertificatesExact data, enclosures, checkers, and proved checker soundness
BridgeImport conventions, reductions, certified reconstruction, and composition between models
DesignOperating specifications and concrete certified candidates

Do not introduce additional project axioms. Put physical laws in structures/typeclasses and invoke them as hypotheses. Do not manufacture universal instances for empirical material laws. Prove simple consistent instances and existence witnesses to guard against vacuity. Allow the usual logical axioms already used by Lean/Mathlib; “no new domain axioms” is the relevant architectural restriction.

For each theorem record: declaration; source locator; hypotheses; units; boundary and regularity; norm; quantifiers; model; computational errors; empirical inputs; and remaining gaps. No scientific result becomes established merely by appearing in a typeclass field.

Corrections to the starting notes

The original notes provide valuable directions, but the following refinements are necessary before formalization.

Starting claim or suggestionPrecise replacement
Pressure surfaces “must be nested tori”Force balance implies tangency. A connected, compact, orientable regular level surface carrying a nonvanishing tangent field has Euler characteristic zero, hence genus one. Compactness, absence of boundary, nonvanishing field, and connectedness must be checked. This does not produce a global nested foliation.
Global nonsingular Boozer coordinatesUse a flux annulus and circle-valued angles; treat the magnetic axis and any separatrix separately. Existence and regularity of the chart are theorem obligations.
p = 0 is the vacuum limitZero pressure implies force-free balance J × B = 0. Vacuum additionally requires J = 0. A multiply connected vacuum region can carry nontrivial circulation without a single-valued scalar potential.
Exact quasisymmetry universally impossible in an asymmetric deviceExtract the actual Garren–Boozer compatibility/overdetermination result for its chosen expansion and hypotheses. Counting equations alone is not a general impossibility proof. Distinguish different definitions of quasisymmetry. See Garren–Boozer and Burby et al..
Mercier positivity certifies plasma stabilityState the exact local/asymptotic interchange criterion and sign convention. It does not cover every global, free-boundary, resistive, or kinetic mode.
A positive lowest discretized eigenvalue proves continuum stabilityFor a conforming trial space the minimum Rayleigh quotient is an upper bound on the continuum infimum. Certify omitted modes, cross terms, coefficients, boundary terms, and equilibrium error before claiming a lower bound.
δW > 0 for every nonzero displacement is the automatic goalNeutral/relabeling modes and continuous spectrum may preclude strict coercivity. Choose the quotient, observable, or nonnegative-energy conclusion supported by the model.
KAM ensures absolute persistence of nested surfacesAppropriate KAM theorems preserve selected sufficiently nonresonant invariant tori under quantitative hypotheses. They do not fill the region with tori or guarantee particle confinement.
A radii polynomial needs a “negative root”Require an admissible positive radius at which the appropriate polynomial is negative, plus contraction, domain, and fixed-point-to-equation conditions.
Filament curvature and example clearances ensure constructionInclude finite winding packs, ribbon strain, forces, supports, heat, irradiation, ports, and tolerances. Source limits from the chosen material and machine.
Stellarators are immune to current-driven disruptionsThey do not require a large inductive plasma current for confinement. Finite plasma currents and multiple instability mechanisms remain possible.

The BFKK paper includes interface and vacuum terms; reproduce the appropriate full boundary formulation before extending a fixed-boundary result.

The model hierarchy

Select a model by the phenomenon being certified, rather than making ideal MHD explain every effect.

ModelPrincipal useAdditional obligations
Magnetostatics with specified currentsCoil fields, forces, vacuum geometryFinite conductor corrections, boundary/circulation data
Static isotropic MHDPressure/current equilibrium and large-scale stabilityDomain, boundary, pressure/current profiles, regularity, model-validity regime
Resistive/two-fluid/anisotropic MHDReconnection, diffusion, pressure anisotropy, flowsClosure, dissipation, singular perturbation and wall models
Guiding-centre dynamicsDrift-orbit and energetic-particle confinementSmall gyroradius ordering, nonvanishing field, reduction error
Drift-kinetic/Fokker–PlanckNeoclassical transport, bootstrap current, ambipolar electric fieldCollision operator, boundary conditions, conservation, profiles
Gyrokinetic dynamicsMicroinstability and turbulent transportOrdering, field equations, energy/free energy, nonlinear closure
Vlasov–Maxwell with specified collisions/sourcesHigher-fidelity kinetic referenceWell-posedness, high-dimensional analysis, reduction theorems
Heat/elasticity/circuit/neutron transportComponent and reactor feasibilityEmpirical constitutive laws, coupled source envelopes, life history

Full-orbit Newton–Lorentz dynamics and Vlasov–Maxwell are also models; do not call one an exact statement of all physical reality. Reduction arrows require quantified theorems or explicit supplied error envelopes.

Certificate composition

Let a describe coil/plasma/component geometry; let θ ∈ U collect correlated operating, manufacturing, and material parameters. The useful robust statement is typically

$$\forall\theta\in U,\quad \exists e_\theta,\quad \operatorname{Equilibrium}(a,\theta,e_\theta) \land \operatorname{Stability}(e_\theta,\theta) \land \operatorname{Transport}(e_\theta,\theta) \land \operatorname{Components}(a,e_\theta,\theta).$$

All parts must use the same witness eθ. Independent proofs of ∃ e, Equilibrium e ∧ Stable e and ∃ e, Equilibrium e ∧ Transport e do not guarantee a common witness. Prefer a unique gauge-fixed equilibrium branch where possible, or carry the witness through dependent certificate records.

Suppose a proved reference form qθ₀ has margin c>0 in norm V. Identify all forms on the same admissible space, using a proved pullback when domains change. If a second theorem proves, for L≥0,

$$|q_\theta(\xi)-q_{\theta_0}(\xi)|\le L\|\theta-\theta_0\|\|\xi\|_V^2,$$

and ‖θ-θ₀‖≤R for every θ∈U, then LR<c yields the robust margin c-LR>0. A diameter bound is an alternative only when U is bounded and θ₀∈U. The existence of such a norm-compatible perturbation estimate is a mathematical target, not an automatic consequence of continuity.

First implementation targets

  1. Define a small algebraic equilibrium interface with μ₀ > 0 and exact force balance; prove field/current tangency.
    Progress so far: Partially implemented — algebraic force balance and tangency; positive permeability and actual differential laws in the restricted slab model.
  2. Construct uniform-field, constant-pressure witnesses and a nonzero-current force-free example.
    Progress so far: Lean verified — uniform-field and nonzero-current force-free witnesses in the Cartesian slab model; not toroidal confinement.
  3. Define the certificate/assumption record without any physics-specific global theorem.
    Progress so far: Partially implemented — common-witness contracts and an assumption ledger; full decoded candidate identity remains pending.
  4. Formalize exact interval containment and one useful geometric or thermal bound.
    Progress so far: Partially implemented — interval containment, continuous clearance, and exact whole-box slab margin; text parsing and general signed multiplication remain pending.
  5. Define an abstract symmetric energy form and prove the elementary conservation/coercivity theorem before implementing the continuum MHD bridge.
    Progress so far: Partially implemented — conditional conservation and coercivity bounds for continuous symmetric forms on one normed space; no MHD PDE bridge.
