# Implementation plan, note corrections and proof-engineering skill — 20 October 2026

## Outcome

A planning and infrastructure run. **No new physics theorem was proved.** The library still has the 14 October results, plus one reference module.

- **Implementation plan** ([notes/implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md)). Work proceeds in cycles across three layers: theory tracks T1–T10, certificate techniques, and a model ladder M0–M6. The ladder runs from the existing exact symmetric equilibria to an integrated candidate, and each rung proves properties for **both** goals: confinement (G1), and keeping the plasma hot for long enough (G2). G2 was absent from the library, so the plan adds kernel concepts for radial heat transport and D-T power balance.
- **Corrections to recent notes,** recorded in plan §1 and to be applied to the notes in Cycle 1:
  - A subharmonic |B|² rules out an interior **maximum** of |B| in vacuum, not a minimum. Minimum-B vacuum fields exist.
  - The magnetic 2-form vanishes on flux surfaces, so the line bundle there is flat. A positive degree is wrong.
  - Global regularity of 2-D/helical *ideal* MHD is open.
  - Several kernel contracts proposed in the notes are vacuous unless a theorem produces them.
- **Proof-engineering skill** `plasmalean-lean-proofs`. It covers `grind` and `fun_induction`, and uses `bv_decide` only for Bool/BitVec goals. Measured here: when the SAT solver runs, `bv_decide` adds a native axiom that the audit rejects. Large certificates are built as explicit proof terms (the `SubsetSum` pattern) rather than by `decide`.
- **`PlasmaLean/SubsetSum.lean`** is now part of the build, as the reference example for that pattern.

**Obstruction recorded.** 3.5 MeV fusion alphas are **not** covered in either equilibrium. In the Solov'ev case their normalized rigidity is about 1/31, against the 1/64 required. The crude canonical-momentum band confines them only to ψ̃ ≲ 0.16, beyond the pressure boundary ψ̃ = 1/16. Under symmetry breaking, the full-orbit defect bound gives times ∝ 1/(δ|v|), a ballistic time scale far shorter than slowing-down times. Practical alpha confinement in a quasisymmetric stellarator needs guiding-centre averaging and orbit-width theory (roadmap R4, R6).

### Corrections and challenged claims

- The Clebsch-torus field was first planned to have positive vertical curl throughout its region. That is false: the vertical curl changes sign at R² = 4/3, inside the region. Non-vacuum status is proved with the horizontal components instead.
- The screw-pinch exploration showed that p ∝ (1 − r²) or (1 − r²)² always violates Suydam near the axis, because p′ ∼ r while the shear term ∼ r³. The certified profile uses p ∝ (1 − r⁴).
- The helical equilibrium formula continues to negative pressure outside ψ_b. Setting p = 0 there with the same field is **not** an equilibrium, since (∇×B)×B ≠ 0, so a current sheet and a different exterior field would be needed. Claims are restricted to ψ ≤ ψ_b, which the covered orbits never leave. The orbit hypothesis is still stated for the smooth field on all of space.
- A helically symmetric rotating-ellipse family with F = −2ψ is also an equilibrium, but its field vanishes on the axis. The run used the second family (F = 4 + 2ψ, μ₀p′ = −4).
