# Phase 4 (exploratory research): Synthesis

Consolidate the exploration into `<task>/05_research_brief.md`.

1. **Gather the artifacts:** `01_problem_formalization.md`, `02_research_questions.md`,
   `02_literature_review.md` if present, and `04_research_trace.md`.
2. **Separate status levels**, using the project's evidence states:
   - facts proved in the trace, sourced theorems with locators, and verified project
     declarations, each labelled as such;
   - plausible conjectures (`stated`);
   - refuted candidates, with their counterexamples;
   - open questions;
   - examples and counterexamples.
3. **Example ledger:** a table of representative examples, counterexamples and boundary cases, and
   the hypotheses each one tests.
4. **Candidate theorems:** for each surviving conjecture, a precise theorem-style statement and its
   missing proof obligations, ready to become a question or note in the project graph.
5. **Next work:** three to five recommended probes or proof attempts, each a possible
   `plasmalean-try-to-prove` or `plasmalean-explore-mathematics` task.
6. **Avoid overclaiming:** never present a conjecture as an established result.

For structured output, follow `../schemas/11-research-synthesis.json`.
