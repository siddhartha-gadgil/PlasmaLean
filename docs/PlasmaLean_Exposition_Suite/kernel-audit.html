✓ Auditor's Checklist

  • No axiom, sorry or decide in the kernel; laws are Prop classes and structures.
  • Each law has an instance, a counterexample and risk-specific checks (the sanity triad).
  • Totalized operations (x / 0 = 0, fderiv of a non-differentiable map) are guarded where a law needs them, and recorded where not (F-3).
  • Region-based laws can be vacuous on an empty region (V-1): look for a witness point.
  • A claim citing ForceBalance must also prove g = ∇p (E-1).
