## Corrected finding — coil geometry and the meridian half-space

**Medium, documentation.** AV-M1 and the modules that cite it said that every coil-generated field fails the whole-meridian-half-space hypothesis. At geometric angle zero the half-space is exactly x > 0. A closed circular filament of radius one centred at (−2,0,0) is entirely outside it. The closed-filament vacuum theorem applies on that entire open half-space, including for unit current. For any orthogonal placement the bound on the first coordinate is centreₓ + |R|. Even equality to zero suffices because the half-space is open. The new regressions are `placedCircle_outside_meridian_zero`, `placedCircle_vacuum_meridian_zero` and `unitCoil_vacuum_meridian_zero`. [Coil regression](../../PlasmaLean/Sanity/MeridianCoil.lean)

## N-51 — what survives a change of model

- **The annular calculation is closed.** `VolumeChart` supplies smoothness on an open collar extending past both edges, a positive density and period, both deck periods, and the two separate infinitesimal volume identities for the fast field and perturbation. Fast invariance makes the density phase-independent. Integrating the perturbation equation gives a closed one-form. On an increasing-angle core the period is minus the mean radial volume flux. Nonzero period rules out a lifted winding-one orbit of the averaged field. Tangency at both boundaries and a nowhere-zero mean on the whole closed band give a normalized stream with nonzero differential. Uniform radial drift gives period −v. Oscillatory shear has an instantaneous radial component of one at phase zero, zero mean and an exactly conserved corrected radius. [Flux identities and examples](../../PlasmaLean/Theory/Geometry/AnnulusFibreFlux.lean) · [Stream function](../../PlasmaLean/Theory/Geometry/AnnulusStream.lean)
- **The time budget is noncircular.** For the exact lifted equation X₀+εY, differentiating the corrected label gives ε²Yg. An interior birth interval gives a uniform positive label margin δ. The sufficient budget is 2|ε|G + tε²K < δ, with G and K chosen positive before the birth and ε. Every partial solution up to its first boundary hit satisfies the estimate. Periodicity bounds the speed in the lifted band, enabling compact continuation on the finite interval. `exists_inverse_square_orbits` constructs a solution through c/ε² for each sufficiently small nonzero ε. The endpoint at that horizon is included. Neither a physical clock nor numerical values of the constants appear in the conclusion. [Corrector and optimal single-orbit residual](../../PlasmaLean/Theory/Geometry/PeriodicOrbitCorrector.lean) · [Scalar estimate](../../PlasmaLean/Theory/Geometry/AveragedConfinement.lean) · [Corrected stream](../../PlasmaLean/Theory/Geometry/AnnulusConfinement.lean) · [First hit](../../PlasmaLean/Theory/Geometry/AnnulusConfinementBounds.lean) · [Orbit continuation](../../PlasmaLean/Theory/Geometry/AnnulusOrbitExistence.lean)
- **Phase overlap has a deliberately restricted meaning.** `VolumePatch.PhaseMatch` compares the density and the two projected velocity components after a pointwise shift. A scratch Lean example matched patches of unit density and zero projected velocity, with respective periods 1 and 2 and respective phase perturbation speeds 0 and 1. The hypotheses allow both discrepancies. Equality of periods is an additional input to `corrector_overlap`. Lifting full trajectories through arbitrary charts also requires actual smooth coordinate changes and compatible full velocities. The global orbit theorem takes a single `VolumeChart`. [Local descent](../../PlasmaLean/Theory/Geometry/AnnulusFluxDescent.lean)
- **Population, radial error and clock matter independently.** The permitted birth interval 0 < a ≤ b < 1 can have a=b. Its mass under an absolutely continuous radial birth distribution can be zero. A positive-width band can also miss a supplied population. Applying the theorem in a device needs positive quantified coverage, a comparison of physical distance with the annular coordinate and a bound on the physical equation's residual. For an additional term ε²Z the derivative acquires ε²d(ψ+εg)[Z], which must be included over the entire claimed horizon. Periodic averaging supplies the homological equation. For guiding centres, Burby, MacKay and Naik derive the reduced Hamiltonian at fixed longitudinal action with transverse form eβ. In their conventions the derivative of normalized half-action with respect to normalized energy equals half the full bounce period multiplied by √(μ/m). Comparing wells at fixed mirror height alone does not establish a winding level of the fixed-action Hamiltonian. ## N-52 — witness identity and geometric scope

The one-turn chord estimate normalizes by the geometric toroidal velocity. Nonzero velocity follows from `FieldTorus` with positive cylindrical radius. Local C¹ regularity on the compact image gives the required Lipschitz estimate there. Grönwall propagates both equal positions and, by difference quotients, zero section tangents. An irrational shift propagates the corresponding closed condition densely. For a common period the angular increment lies in 2πℤ and is constant along a section. The periodic part of the degree-one lift forces that period to be a multiple of 2π. A globally zero section tangent contradicts winding. The resulting conclusion is a C² smooth embedding. The double cover tests injectivity, and the winding-one parametrization θ−sin θ at zero transform tests immersion. Its image is a regular geometric torus, but its parametrization has a stationary tangent. The same pair therefore carries the strict ray order, zero current for each positive permeability, common transform magnitude ≥ 0.2386, both Boozer hypotheses and the respective finite-mode off-QA defect bounds of 0.0063|ι_C| and 0.0090|ι_A|. This supplies boundary embeddings. A filled flux chart, trapped guiding centres and finite-pressure stability each need further hypotheses.
