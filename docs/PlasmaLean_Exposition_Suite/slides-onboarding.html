The Core Physics Problem

Why Stellarators Are a Pure Geometry Optimization Problem

Tokamaks (2D Axisymmetric)

  • Continuous rotational symmetry $\partial_\phi \mathbf{B} = 0$.
  • Noether's theorem automatically conserves canonical toroidal momentum $P_\phi$, confining particle orbits.
  • Fatal flaw: Requires megiamperes of inductive plasma current $\implies$ violent disruptions & pulsed operation.

Stellarators (3D Non-Axisymmetric)

  • External 3D twisted modular coils create rotational transform $\iota(\psi)$ without driven plasma current.
  • Challenge: Breaking 2D symmetry destroys $P_\phi$ unless the 3D field satisfies Quasisymmetry: $|B(\psi, heta,\phi)| = B(\psi, M heta - N\phi)$.
