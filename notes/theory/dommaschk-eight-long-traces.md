The eight endpoints delimit the two local fixed-action arcs of N-60 on each symmetry sector. Each compressed witness records 391 steps of width 1/64 geometric radian in both directions. There are 6,256 checked steps in total. Each directed trace reaches 391/64 radians measured from its own common section. The radius label is the dimensionless interpolation parameter between the two launch profiles. [Local fixed-action arcs](dommaschk-local-action-graphs.md)

The replay starts from the transferred seed, reconstructs each step from the stored polynomial coefficients and frames, checks the incoming and outgoing radii, and recomputes the archived action and first-mirror crossings. At the second section the sine and cosine harmonics at modes 2 and 6 change sign. The exact checker takes the section from the witness identity. Its replay path disables the floating-point trajectory and frame proposers. The physical Lean theorem in N-61 gives a field line for 1/64 radian from each birth in both sectors. Extending it along these eight long traces requires proving the reference defect and spatial derivative bounds on the moving tubes, then applying the gluing theorem to the transferred physical start. The radial-variation bounds for the 16 tiles form a further dependency of an action-graph theorem. Physical scales and the thermal confinement target remain open.
