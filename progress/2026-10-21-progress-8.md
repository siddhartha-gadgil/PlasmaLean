# Toward all-time confinement of fusion alphas in a stellarator field: an exact guiding-centre invariant torus; coil-KAM blocked — 21 October 2026

## Outcome

This run adds about **75 Lean modules** (many certificate modules). The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 33506 declarations.

- **An exact invariant torus of the guiding-centre field** (`Design.RationalStellaratorGC.gc_exact_torus`, `gc_exact_torus_real`).
  - **Setting:** passing guiding centres with μ = 0 follow field lines of B̃ = B + ρ_s∇×b, by the Littlejohn model (supplied). For the exact stellarator field and ρ_s = 1/256 (3.5 MeV alphas at B₀R₀ ≈ 69 T·m), B̃ has an exact real-analytic invariant torus with the same rotational transform ι = (75 + √5)/562, within 10⁻¹² of a certified approximate torus.
  - **Machinery:**
    - a generic KAM theorem for fields with an inverse-square-root node (`KamFlow*S`, `PolyExprSStrip`, `InvSqrt`, `InvSqrtApprox`);
    - the guiding-centre field's flow form with W²q = 1 exactly (`RationalStellaratorGC*`);
    - ball-arithmetic Kronecker certificates (`KronBall`, `KronBallGrid`, `PolyExprSMajQ`).
  - **Certified constants:** residual ≤ 3.7×10⁻²³; sharp admissibility bounds (twist ≈ 0.44, torsion ≈ 37); node validity maj E ≤ 0.286 < 1; κ ≈ 6×10¹⁰; **smallness 32κe₀/δ₀⁴ ≈ 6.8×10⁻⁴, a margin of about 1470**.
  - **Remaining (in progress):** the cylindrical identity for gcField, C² regularity, the profile and collar, then all-time trapping and existence for B̃, and the transfer to μ = 0 guiding-centre orbits and the SI alpha corollary.
  - **Build cost:** 45 modules, ≈ 35 min sequential, ≤ 5.5 GB per process.
- **Coil-produced all-time confinement: blocked (honest negative).** The analytic infrastructure is built:
  - two-variable Cauchy formula and joint holomorphy;
  - a complexified Biot–Savart coil field with an analytic-field interface;
  - Paley–Wiener / aliasing certificates;
  - a complete sampled-residual pipeline for a refitted coil torus.

  But the certified residual is ≈ 10⁹ against the ≈ 10⁻³⁰ the KAM step needs. The gap comes from a crude complex sup bound (45000 vs ≈ 10 actual), a ψ-strip narrowed to |Im ψ| ≲ 0.13 by coil distance, and the lack of a lower bound on |B^φ| that captures cancellation between filaments. A useful certificate would cost ≈ 4 CPU-days. The demonstration certificate is committed but kept out of the default build (`notes/theory/coil-kam-status.md`). **This route is paused as a research problem.**
