# Phase 7 (part 1): certified field-line confinement in an exact stellarator field; the coarea step formalized — 20 October 2026

## Outcome

Part 1 of Phase 7 of the [implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) adds **7 Lean modules**. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 9921 declarations. The trapped-alpha and all-surface ballooning work of Phase 7 is still in progress and will be reported in part 2.

- **Certified field-line confinement in a genuine stellarator field** (`Design.RationalStellarator.Confined.rationalStellarator_confined`, `_local`). The field is the exact rational vacuum field B = ∇φ + ¼∇h: non-axisymmetric, 2 field periods, stellarator-symmetric. Every field line starting within ≈ 0.023 R₀ of the magnetic axis (s² + z² ≤ (1/44)², s = (x² + y² − 1)/2) stays in the tube |s|, |z| ≤ 1/10 for field-line parameter in [0, 750], and makes **at least 95 full toroidal transits** there.
  - **Method:** an order-10 approximate first integral I (363 monomials, stellarator-symmetric) with |DI·B| ≤ 4×10⁻⁶ on the whole tube, certified by an exact-integer Taylor-shift cover; I > I_b on the faces; I ≤ I_a on the start tube; the general defect-confinement theorem.
  - The local version only needs the curve to be a field line on [0, 750].
  - **Scope:** a finite length (not KAM / all-time), a vacuum field with no plasma, and no coils yet. The field is singular on the z-axis line current, which lies outside the tube.
  - **Build cost:** ≈ 1 min and ≈ 5 GB peak memory. The cover uses monolithic `decide +kernel` checkers because cell re-expansions are shared down the Taylor-shift tree; the reviewer accepted this deviation from the proof-term guidance.
- **The coarea step is formalized** (`Design.SolovevCoarea`), the last stated link in the geometry of the one-plasma theorem. For the reactor Solov'ev coordinates:
  - the coordinate map is injective, its image is the plasma shell, and the axis is null;
  - the Jacobian determinant is √2σ/R;
  - the Lebesgue volume of {s′ ≤ s} equals the coordinate volume;
  - for continuous axisymmetric f, d/ds ∫_{s′≤s} f dx = ∮ f dS/|∇s|.

  `solovev_one_plasma_actual_coarea` bundles this with `solovev_one_plasma_actual`, so the flux-surface averages used in the transport law are now *proved* to be the volume derivatives. Supplied ingredients remain: the transport model (χ, density, sources), the fusion data, and solutions.
