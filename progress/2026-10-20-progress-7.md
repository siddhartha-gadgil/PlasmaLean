# Phase 6: one plasma on the actual geometry, high-Q and ignited, ballooning, a first exact stellarator field — 20 October 2026

## Outcome

Phase 6 of the [implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) adds **13 Lean modules**. The library has no project axioms; the audit covers 9138 declarations.

- **One consistent plasma on the actual geometry.** `Design.SolovevFluxGeometry.solovev_one_plasma_actual` removes the supplied transport-geometry class.
  - V′, ⟨|∇s|²⟩ and ⟨Δs⟩ are built from the exact flux surfaces of the reactor Solov'ev field. The flux-geometry laws are proved, including the Gauss identity (V′g)′ = V′h and the one-sided limits at the axis.
  - The required enclosures are proved for every s ∈ [0, 1], by polynomial squeezes of v^{-3/2} and exact Wallis integrals, with margins of only ≈ 0.26%.
  - The one remaining modelling link is the change of variables identifying these surface integrals with Lebesgue volume (the coarea step), which is stated, not proved.
- **High-Q and ignited versions.** Using 100 quarter-keV Bosch–Hale windows (supplied data, ±1%, recomputed by review) and a √T tangent bound for bremsstrahlung:
  - `solovev_one_plasma_highQ_actual`: ≈ 17.5 MW auxiliary heating, oracle Q ≈ 39–46, χ₀ ∈ [0.78, 0.84], edge offset Δ = 40 kPa, T ≤ 19 keV;
  - `solovev_one_plasma_ignited_actual`: **no auxiliary heating**, but only with Δ = 90 kPa (≈ 20% of the axis pressure; an edge pedestal with a current sheet; at s = 1 the MHD pressure is ≈ 128 kPa against ≈ 12 kPa kinetic) and a ±0.8% χ₀ window. With Δ = 40 kPa the ignited upper barrier exceeds the pressure ceiling.
  - Q values are untrusted oracle numbers; the bands and pressure bounds are theorems.
- **Ideal-ballooning stability of the Solov'ev equilibrium on four near-axis surfaces** (`Design.SolovevBallooning.reactor_ballooning_s1`–`s4`, ψ̃ ∈ {1, 4, 9, 16} out of 625).
  - Covers every field line and every ballooning angle θ_k, using the exact equilibrium coefficients and a new 2-D Riccati certificate.
  - The growing geodesic-curvature drive is proved to be an exact derivative.
  - The ballooning reduction (high-n δW → line energy) is supplied, as the Mercier formula was. Certificate data came from an untrusted SOCP oracle (`scripts/solovev_ballooning_oracle.py`).
- **First genuinely stellarator field** (`Design.RationalStellarator`).
  - An exact rational vacuum field B = ∇φ + ε∇h (ε = 1/4, h a degree-6 harmonic rational function), proved a `VacuumField` on {x² + y² > 0}, with 2 field periods, stellarator symmetry and non-axisymmetry.
  - The unit circle is an exact closed field line.
  - The axis is linearly elliptic: monodromy trace 2cos 2πκ with κ = √3/2, and the field-line Jacobian is proved.
  - A general defect-confinement theorem for approximate first integrals (`Fields.fieldLine_confined_of_defect`).
  - A certified finite-length confinement theorem for this field is in progress.
- **Trapped alphas in near-QA (partial).** Turning points and bounce sequences are proved (exact QA, and near-QA within a flux band), with tip rigidity in exact QA and an exact kick form for periodic reference orbits. **No trapped-alpha confinement time yet:**
  - non-resonance is unproved, and a real resonance (Δφ_b ≈ π) occurs at r₀ ≈ 0.95 m;
  - periodicity of the reference orbit needs ODE uniqueness;
  - the remainder bound is open.
