02 · Reusable mathematical progress

03 / 09

The foundations have executable proof content.

Simple models make the definitions testable before tackling three-dimensional plasma physics.

Lean checked

Equilibrium

Force balance implies field/current tangency to pressure. Uniform and nonzero-current force-free slab examples provide witnesses.

Abstract results

Energy & stability

Conditional energy conservation, coercivity and retained/tail form bounds. A neutral-mode example shows why energy need not control displacement.

Lean checked

Geometry & fields

Continuous clearance, arbitrarily placed circular coils, Biot–Savart integrals and an exact on-axis loop field.

Progress so far

These are compiled elementary results and sanity checks, not just declarations of future tasks.

Claim boundary. Zero pressure does not imply zero current. Abstract energy bounds do not yet establish a continuum MHD stability result.

04 · The concrete candidate so far

05 / 09

Two circular coils. Precisely stated regions.

The first device-specific object is a calibration fixture with symbolic positive reference current I and permeability μ₀. Its field model uses ideal filaments.

Exact inputs

Nominal geometry

Each coil has radius 0.10 m, in the planes z = ±0.20 m, with the same angular orientation.

Full-domain bounds

Two different regions

Field/error/derivative bounds cover the 5 cm observation ball. The positive axial-field certificate covers the smaller 1 mm probe ball.

Progress so far

Closure, regularity, one-turn simplicity, source separation and the nominal midpoint field have proofs.

Claim boundary. The diagram is schematic, not a field-line simulation or a construction drawing. Neither ball is a certified confinement region.

05 · What survives manufacturing uncertainty

06 / 09

Margins for the same perturbed coils and currents.

Assume closed C¹ coils with position error ≤ 1 mm, independent tangent error ≤ 1 mm/radian, and independent current errors ≤ 1% of I.

Lean checked

Field error

Actual minus nominal field has norm at most μ₀I/4 throughout the 5 cm observation ball (SI lengths in metres).

Lean checked

Nonzero probe field

The actual axial component is at least μ₀I/4 throughout the 1 mm probe ball, for μ₀ > 0 and I > 0.

Lean checked

Tube clearance

The two 5 mm-radius envelopes around actual coil centrelines remain at least 97/250 m = 388 mm apart.

Progress so far

Manufacturing and current errors compose into continuous field and clearance certificates, not just sampled checks.

Claim boundary. These tolerances are invented test inputs. Tube envelopes do not prove embedded winding packs or a finite-conductor electromagnetic model.

06 · Latest run: a stronger result and a sharper limit

07 / 09

Spatial derivatives are proved. Confinement is not.

The latest run added seven modules and 51 source theorems, including continuous field differentiation, pose/tolerance interfaces and a probe-escape sanity result.

Lean checked

Actual field derivatives

Dominated differentiation gives the true spatial Fréchet derivative and an operator-norm bound throughout the observation ball.

Lean checked

A useful countercheck

For a classical field line γ′(s) = B(γ(s)) staying in the probe ball on [0, T], the same assumptions imply Tμ₀I ≤ 1/125. It cannot remain there forever.

Progress so far

A nonvanishing-field certificate now comes with an explicit check against mistaking it for all-time trapping in the probe ball.

Claim boundary. The parameter s has units m/T, not seconds or particle time. This result does not rule out confinement elsewhere or prove trajectory existence. Source-free Maxwell identities remain to be proved.

07 · From calibration to a stellarator

08 / 09

The remaining device work is explicit.

The chosen first product is a small vacuum-field demonstrator. A four-circular-coil, CNT-inspired architecture is a reference direction, not a completed replica or a certified design.

Reference only

VD1 · Freeze the candidate

Version the four coil placements, frames, radii, turns/current conventions and leads against a source dataset.

Partial: fixture only

VD2 · Continuous fields

Extend the fixture's geometry/derivative work to the candidate and prove source-free Maxwell identities.

Not certified

VD3–4 · Conductors & confinement

Model embedded finite winding packs, then certify an invariant barrier or a declared finite-time orbit bundle.

Not qualified

VD5 · Physical operation

Source conductor, supply, thermal, mechanical, protection and metrology envelopes for that same candidate.

Progress so far

Reusable mathematics supports partial P2 work. No practical build or energization specification has been released.

Claim boundary. Finite-pressure equilibrium, all-mode MHD, transport and reactor engineering are separate later obligations, not implied by a vacuum demonstrator.
