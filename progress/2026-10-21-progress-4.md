# Phase 9 (part 4): the finite-β non-axisymmetric families are Mercier-unstable — 21 October 2026

## Outcome

This run adds **2 Lean modules** with a negative stability finding. The library has no project axioms.

- **Landreman family 1 is Mercier-unstable on every flux surface** (`Design.LandremanMercier.family1_mercier_unstable`).
  - Explicit field-line coordinates show the Jacobian is constant, so V″ = 0 (neither well nor hill).
  - A general new lemma (`Theory.Stability.MercierShearless.mercierIndex_neg_of_forceBalance_Vpp_zero`, via a strict Cauchy–Schwarz inequality) shows that with zero shear, no well and ∇p ≠ 0, force balance forces D_Merc < 0.
  - The closed-line interchange integral ∮dl/|B| is constant (2π), so the closed-line criterion is only marginal. Mercier's asymptotics are degenerate at zero shear, and the docstrings record this.
- **Landreman family 2 (ε = 1/4, S = 4) is Mercier-unstable numerically**, driven by a magnetic hill forced by the positive shear (V′ ∝ ι, derived by hand and checked numerically but not formalized).
  - `family2_mercier_unstable_of_enclosures` proves instability on a surface *given* numerical enclosures (≈ 2.5× slack) as hypotheses. No validated quadrature exists for family 2 yet.
  - For λ = 50 the outer surface comes out Mercier-stable (fixture).
- **The source paper (arXiv:2609.26742 v2) makes no stability claims.** These verdicts are local interchange only and rest on the supplied Landreman–Jorge (2020) Mercier formula.

**Consequence for the plan:** the library's finite-β non-axisymmetric equilibria are not MHD-stable stellarator candidates as they stand. A stable finite-β stellarator rung still has to come from elsewhere: the stepped-pressure-on-KAM-torus route, or parameter regions like the λ = 50 outer surface.
